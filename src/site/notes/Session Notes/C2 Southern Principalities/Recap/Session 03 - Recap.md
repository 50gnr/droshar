# Southern Principalities — Session Record

## Session 3 — The Rapids Turn Red

The theft at the Shrine of the Rapids immediately became a fight for survival.

As the Ryūtō swarms rose from the river, Genpei joined them in trying to recover the stolen offering bundles and drive the party away from the shrine. Nobu called for Clay to remove Genpei from the fight, and the old kappa quickly became Clay’s sole focus.

What followed was a brutal engagement across the bridge and river.

Nobu took the worst of it. Over the course of the fight, the party pulled him back from unconsciousness or death again and again—through healing, polymorph, reversed moments, magnetic intervention, and anything else they could bring to bear. Whatever luck had followed him into the shrine did not follow him out.

The Ryūtō and Genpei continued fighting for the offerings rather than pursuing the party beyond the shrine, but by the end of the battle Genpei was dead and the party still held the stolen bundles.

His body was taken with them.

Back aboard the wagon, the victory did not feel especially clean. Sho was openly frustrated that the operation had drifted away from the original plan and laid much of that frustration at Nobu’s feet.

The conversation afterward returned repeatedly to the same point:

> Slow down.  
> Take the time to understand the site.  
> Do the job properly.

The wagon is now headed for the **Farmer’s Shrine**.

The current idea is to use Maki as the distraction while the others take a more deliberate approach to the theft.

## Current Position

- Shrine of the Rapids offerings secured
- Genpei dead
- Genpei’s body aboard the wagon
- Nobu survived a very bad night
- Party en route to the Farmer’s Shrine
- Maki likely to serve as the primary distraction
- The group intends to move slower and plan more carefully this time
