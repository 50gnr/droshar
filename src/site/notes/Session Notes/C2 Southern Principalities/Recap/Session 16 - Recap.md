# Southern Principalities — Session Record

## Session 16 Recap — The Wedding Enquiry

The morning at Maki's shop began with papers, aliases, and an engagement nobody entirely agreed was happening. Maki would appear as himself; Maeva became **Lady Annabelle**, his bride-to-be; Sho used **Vladimir**, Annabelle's brother and increasingly reluctant financier. Clay was **Cray the Third**, Maki's bodyguard, while Nobu adopted **Ubon** and the role of wedding planner. Remy helped Maki produce a candy engagement ring. Nobu assembled a spectacular outfit from imported fashions, complete with a plumed hat and a cloak that somehow made the colours work together. Sho chose quieter clothes and sunglasses to conceal the glow of Detect Magic. Before they left, the group raised a less decorative problem: getting out of locked-down In'nali after killing its High Steward. Papers and a convincing relationship would help them circulate, but neither supplied an escape plan.

At the **High Circles Reception Hall**, Sayo Umehara welcomed the five visitors for their wedding consultation. Preparations were underway for the political supper in two days. The front desk was less welcoming. **Etsu**, a dwarf responsible for recording arrivals and departures, checked their names and began questioning their wedding planner. Ubon claimed to have arrived only a couple of days earlier, despite the closed Great Lift and city lockdown. Maki explained that his connections had brought them in by air, refused to name those connections, and threatened to take his business elsewhere when the questions continued. His indignation kept the visit moving. It did not clear the suspicion: Etsu said she would pass the name to **Iori**, the house mage, for further checking.

Sayo took them upstairs to a private parlour, where the supposed couple supplied a romance involving a spa, a helpful rescue, and a meddling kappa. Annabelle wanted colour, excellent food, and nothing too good for her Maki; Vladimir wanted to know what any of it would cost. Their questions repeatedly returned to security. Sayo described magical screening, watches for invisible guests, magically secured doors, spell wardens, clear-sight specialists, a private suite protected with Sanctum, and longer watches supported by a casting circle or witness glass. Harmful or controlling magical items could be held in the coat room or vault during a screened event, while harmless items might remain with their owners. Such screening was a paid event service, explaining why the consultation had not stopped their own magic at the door. Their running security estimate reached **11,200 gold**, before the venue and catering. They had learned what could be purchased; Sayo did **not** know which protections had been selected for the political supper.

Between the consultation and the tour, the crew gained a useful picture of the building. The banquet hall rose beneath a glass roof, with an upper gallery overlooking it; dressing rooms, storage, and offices occupied the upper level. The main entrance and rear loading bay were the building's two outside access points, and service corridors and stairs carried staff between their work areas. A hurried messenger delivered security reports behind a closed door, prompting a quiet request to remember his face. Sayo continued trying to arrange an actual wedding: guests, colours, customs, and a date after the supper. Maki offered candy, and a fish-head-breaking ceremony joined the proposed festivities. The more extravagant the requests became, the more useful Vladimir's anxiety about paying for them sounded.

Sho used a visit to the washroom to approach the manager's office. The older lizardfolk inside gave Vladimir five minutes, admitting him behind a door whose locks reset themselves and whose frame flashed with an arcane sigil. The manager, **Ren**, explained that the venue alone usually cost **15,000 gold**. Sho claimed an overall ceiling of **25,000** and pleaded to discuss it away from his demanding sister. Ren agreed to make that figure work, with Maki's position as Master of Fisheries lending the possibility of useful future arrangements. He required **5,000 gold up front**, with the remaining **20,000 due on the wedding evening**. The conversation then became more dangerous. Staying in character as the disapproving brother, Sho suggested he would welcome the relationship failing and floated having Maki framed. Ren was interested enough to invite another conversation. Sho backed away from the specifics and asked to leave. He had found an opening, but also given the manager something compromising to remember.

Back in the tasting lounge, Chef **Daigo** presented an elaborate tray of pastries, cakes, and sweets. Maeva's Annabelle chose a banana-cream Danish as her favourite. Nobu tried to pocket samples until Daigo caught him and explained that he could simply ask for a takeaway box. The bride's insistence that milk was a problem but cream was acceptable ended in a disastrous rush to the washroom. Passing Sho outside the manager's office, Maeva pushed the still-unlatched door open on the pretext of mistaking it for the bathroom, then hurried away when Ren redirected her. The commotion provided another glimpse inside, rather than an opportunity to search it. Even afterward, Annabelle remained committed to the banana pastries. Some parts of the cover were becoming expensive to maintain.

The final inspection reached Daigo's kitchen door. He refused to let the visitors wander through his working kitchen, but allowed them to look in from the threshold: a long galley, an island, and a small team busy with preparation, dishes, and the stove. The group left with **two boxes of pastries** and substantially more knowledge of the venue. Sayo had also learned that Vladimir had gone over her head on the price, and warned that bypassing her could have drawbacks. The supposed wedding would still need its deposit by the following day, before the political event. **No deposit had been paid.** No wedding had taken place, and none of the quoted security services had been put into operation for them.

By early afternoon, the consultation was over and the assassination was still ahead. Maki and Maeva intended to attend the supper as the politician and his future wife, with Clay accompanying them as personal security. Nobu wanted to find a catering employee who could be persuaded to arrange a replacement shift, letting him return in a different mask, outfit, and manner. Sho planned another conversation with Ren, using the strained engagement and difficulty paying as a possible route into the event. Both conversations remained to be played; neither a catering position nor Ren's help had been secured. The crew also still had to reconcile their invitations, decide whether the wedding cover justified a 5,000-gold deposit, and settle how they would actually reach Aketsu Blackwing. They had obtained a venue tour, lunch, and a proposal for an exceptionally well-protected wedding. The operation still needed a plan everyone could afford to follow.

## Decisions and Consequences

- The wedding consultation gave all five members a legitimate visit to the High Circles Reception Hall. It did not give all five admission to the political supper.
- Their cover identities were Maki, Lady Annabelle (**Maeva**), Vladimir (**Sho**), Ubon (**Nobu**), and Cray the Third (**Clay**).
- **Etsu questioned Ubon's account of entering the locked-down city** and said she would refer it to Iori. Maki's intervention kept the appointment going; the promised vetting remained unresolved.
- The crew learned the venue's general layout and available magical-security services. The supper's actual security package remained unconfirmed.
- Ren conditionally agreed to a **25,000-gold wedding**, requiring **5,000 down** and **20,000 on the evening**. The deposit was deferred, **not paid**, and the booking was not yet secured.
- Sho's private conversation left Ren aware that the supposed bride's brother might welcome trouble for Maki. It produced an invitation to talk again, not an agreement to frame or harm anyone.
- The party took away two boxes of pastries. They did not gain unrestricted kitchen access, a staff appointment, or access to the house mage.

## Current Situation

The group had left the High Circles Reception Hall in the early afternoon. Aketsu Blackwing was alive, and the political supper was still ahead. Maki and Maeva planned to use the invited guest route, with Clay as Maki's guard; the invitation details still needed checking. Nobu's catering approach and Sho's follow-up with Ren were pending. No one had yet arranged those additional entries.

Ren expected the **5,000-gold deposit by the following day** if the wedding was to proceed. The party had not paid it or settled how to fund it. Etsu's suspicions and the house mage's possible vetting remained live concerns. The kill method and a dependable route out of In'nali were unresolved.
