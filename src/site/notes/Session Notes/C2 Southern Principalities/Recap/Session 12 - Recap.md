# Southern Principalities — Session Record

## Session 12 Recap — Run the Line

Noon found the party in three places. Clay stood among the dead at the old stone circle, with four [[Root Wardens\|Root Wardens]] still rooted in separate beds. Across the [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Hakuryū Valley\|Hakuryū]], Sho and Maeva had the [[Accord Bell\|Accord Bell]] and had brought Nobu out of the clearing by Dimension Door. He was alive, stable, and unconscious beneath the riverside brush. Farther east, Maki carried the Heart along an overgrown trail toward the [[Quiet Fox\|Quiet Fox]]'s warehouse. The three Ebizo were rowing after him, while the green Oniborne searched overhead.

The first problem was the pursuer in the sky. Clay took aim from the original bank and struck the Oniborne hard enough to send him into the river. The current swept him from view. No one saw him surface, but no one recovered a body either. His fate remained unknown.

The Ebizo made shore and followed Maki's route. Sho and Maeva used invisibility to let them pass, then took the now-empty boat back across the river to help Clay. Before leaving, they separated from the magic that had briefly joined them for the rescue; the parting brought lethargy, though one of them used Greater Restoration to clear it. Nobu remained hidden and stable, not dead or making death saves.

### Four Hands and No Oxen

Clay had meanwhile begun doing what the party's plan had neglected: moving the Wardens. He tore at the roots binding the first one into the soil. Its great hand twisted and reached toward him as the roots snapped. Beneath the clearing, the four had begun to grow into one another again. The farther Clay pulled one from its neighbours, the more it quieted; brought close, the fingers and fresh tendrils reached back. He eventually freed all four and returned each to its own soil-filled cart, spaced apart enough to make them still.

There was a practical hitch. The party had killed the oxen during the raid. They had four enormous living forearms, four two-wheeled carts, a boat, and a warehouse a day's walk toward [[An'nali\|An'nali]]—but no teams to pull the load. Their discussion ranged from floating the Wardens to carrying them, and from retracing the road to letting the arms crawl after one another. The latter experiment taught them that the hands could grasp a person as readily as a neighbouring Warden. It did not give them a safe route through the jungle.

Once the geography was clear, the party chose the river. They ferried the Wardens across separately, with the carts following, and arranged them with gaps on the far bank. The crossing took well over an hour. They also loaded the still-unconscious Nobu into a cart for the journey. Even ten feet apart, the Wardens sent tendrils along the ropes joining their carts. As the group dragged the train along the grown-in trail, those ropes became thick with new roots. They had to stop repeatedly, sever the growth, retie the carts, and mend their dwindling rope. Moving the Array had become less like hauling cargo than tending a stubborn, living machine.

Maki, meanwhile, was still ahead with the Heart. He laid a false trail and caltrops to delay the Ebizo, but they tracked on. Running on aged, burning lungs, he discovered that panic could finally draw a protective spell from him: a small magical hut in the brush. The pursuers found it. They struck its walls without breaking through, then waited outside. Maki gained shelter and time, but he had also pinned himself in place until his friends could catch up.

### The Custodians Catch Up

The Warden carts made enough noise to betray their approach. As the party neared Maki, the Ebizo elder left the vigil at the hut and came through the trees. A spear struck Clay, and the elder closed with his heavy pincers. The other two followed. The elder demanded the Heart back; the party carrying the carts did not have it, and Maki was still sheltering with it farther along the trail.

The fight spilled around the carts. Maeva kept a Moonbeam on the attackers, at times catching Clay at its edge. Ink burned and blinded him, but his sense of movement through the ground let him keep fighting at close range. Sho sent his tiny origami helpers to aid Clay, then filled the clearing with the heads of a sand hydra. From inside his hut, Maki conjured a great magical hand to punch an Ebizo who had been guarding it.

The elder fell to Sho's sand hydra, its heads breaking through the cracked shell. The two remaining Ebizo fought on, then tried to flee; the party chased them down. By the end, all three pursuers were dead. After a short rest and a brief, irreverent discussion of whether their enemies counted as supper, the party returned to the carts. The Wardens had already rooted into the ground and begun reaching for one another again. They cut them free a second time and resumed the slow haul east.

### Beneath the Warehouse

The group eventually reached a weathered warehouse with sagging roof and broad loading doors. From outside, it looked abandoned. Inside, a stairwell opened through the floor into a chamber vastly larger than the building above it. Black iron braced the walls. Chains and conduits hung toward a central platform. There stood a restraint frame sized for something far larger than a person, its inward-facing clamps scored from within. Grooves in the floor led away from it in circles and angular lines.

The place was not ordinary storage. What the party saw suggested a facility made to hold a powerful spirit in place and subject it to something they did not yet understand.

As the four Wardens were brought inside, the Quiet Fox's note warmed and changed. Its new instruction was to **set up the Array and leave no trace of the Lotus Choir**. The party placed the Wardens, set the Heart at the centre, and left the Bell aside. They did **not** ring it or test the Array again. They considered their delivery complete, but the purpose of the chamber beneath them—and what would happen when the Array was used there—remained unanswered.

## Decisions and Consequences

- Sho and Maeva got Nobu safely across the river. He remained stable but unconscious during the extraction.
- Clay knocked the Oniborne into the Hakuryū. He disappeared downstream; his death was **not** confirmed.
- The Ebizo followed Maki and the Heart rather than the Bell. All three were later killed when they intercepted the reunited party.
- Clay freed all four Root Wardens. The party ferried them across the river and hauled them to the warehouse in separate carts, repeatedly cutting their regrowing connections.
- Maki reached a new spell under pressure and sheltered with the Heart in a magical hut until the party caught up.
- The party delivered the Wardens, Heart, and Bell to the warehouse. The note's final instruction was to set up the Array and leave no trace of the Lotus Choir.
- They saw the restraint facility hidden beneath the warehouse, but did not learn its full purpose or activate the Array there.

## Current Situation

- The **four Root Wardens**, **Heart**, and **Accord Bell** are at the warehouse. The Wardens and Heart were positioned for the Array; the Bell was left aside and **not rung**.
- A vast restraint chamber lies beneath the warehouse. The party has seen its frame, clamps, chains, and floor channels, but does not know what is meant to be held there.
- **Nobu is alive and stable**. He was unconscious throughout the session; his later recovery was not played out.
- The **three Ebizo are dead**. The **Oniborne's fate is unknown** after the river swept him away.
- The party believes its delivery job is done. The consequences of the raid and the facility's use remain unresolved.

## Memorable Table Moments

- Clay discovered that the Wardens could grasp people, then tried using one giant hand to demonstrate the point. The resulting debate over whether anyone had said “please” lasted almost as long as the transport plan.
- The party had to solve a problem of its own making: four carts, no oxen, and a river between them and the destination.
- Maki's panicked spell gave him a sanctuary the Ebizo could not enter—and a conspicuous place for them to wait him out.
- Moonbeam, ink, a fist-shaped spell, Sho's origami helpers, and a sand hydra turned the escort's last stand into an unusually messy rescue.
