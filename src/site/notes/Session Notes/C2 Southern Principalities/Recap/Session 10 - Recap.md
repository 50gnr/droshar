# Southern Principalities — Session Record

## Session 10 Recap — Follow the Thread

The party had six days before four nursery carts left [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Bardune/Rootglass Conservatory\|Rootglass Conservatory]]. Somewhere beneath their ordinary canvas covers would be the Anchors of the [[Compendium/Party/Quests/Stillwater Array\|Stillwater Array]]. The carts would depart separately, follow different roads out of [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Bardune/Bardune\|Bardune]], and eventually converge with the Array's Heart and Accord Bell. The Quiet Fox needed the party to follow them without interrupting the delivery or revealing that anyone knew the Array existed.

Before the clock began, everyone returned to the Shigure baths and accepted the Kappa's invitation to a friendly game of Liar's Dice. The Kappa denied cheating before anyone accused him. Ayame examined the cups, examined the Kappa, and quietly left the room. Dice vanished beneath lacquered cups, bids climbed beyond reason, and the evening gave the party a rare stretch of laughter before another mission built around secrets, pursuit, and consequences.

The courier's dropped schedule remained their one physical connection to the carts. Maki used **Ryoku's Revelation** to follow the paper backward through its own history. The page remembered Rootglass: dark green ink, four route lines beginning at the eastern service yard, and departures spaced exactly twelve minutes apart. It also remembered the elderly, pink-petaled Hanamori who had explained the separation.

> “Because the Root Wardens still reach for one another. If the fingers turn, widen the distance. If the roots break soil, stop the cart and send for me immediately.”

For the first time, the party had a name for the Anchors. They also understood that the staggered routes were more than operational caution. Even dormant and separated, the Root Wardens could sense one another.

### Mending What Time Left Behind

The party's earlier interference with history had accumulated into twelve points of paradox. Sho knew that leaving the damage alone meant gambling on what would break at dawn, so the group paid **1,000 gp** and joined him in a time-mending ritual.

Sho formed a ring of sand and gems and drew the fracture into view. Grains flowed against gravity and vanished into a crack that did not belong in the room. Around him, the others found their own ways to hold the ritual together: rhythm, remembered history, physical force, and the stubborn insistence that this version of their lives remain real.

The ritual succeeded. Three points of paradox were stripped away, reducing the total from **12 to 9**. The cost was not only gold. Every participant surrendered roughly one percent of their natural lifespan, a price that landed uncomfortably close to the expected limit of one party member's life. They had repaired some of the damage, but time had not forgiven them. It had merely accepted payment.

### Six Days in Bardune

During the remaining days, the Quiet Fox delivered the next stage of the assignment: **allow the components to converge, then steal the completed Array by any means and escape with it**. Finding the site was no longer the end of the mission. It was only the approach.

The party spread out to learn what waited along that approach. Clay ranged through the mountain shrine country and observed Oni caretakers maintaining the heights. In Bardune, others watched Rootglass Conservatory, one of the oldest Hanamori institutions in the region. Its ancient central tree stood beneath curved glass while researchers, healers, gardeners, and patients moved through a place devoted to botanical study and rejuvenation. The party treated it as a functioning sanctuary rather than an enemy fortress; one of them even donated **100 gp** to its work.

Piko was sent to watch the eastern service yard. He found nothing outwardly sinister. Three or four wagons passed through on an ordinary day carrying herbs, balms, remedies, and healing potions. That normal traffic was precisely what made four more nursery carts so easy to hide.

On the appointed evening, the party assembled outside the yard. Watering ended. Lanterns were hooded. The first cart emerged into the dark, followed by another every twelve minutes. Each had a driver, a caretaker, and two guards walking alongside it. The carts and oxen appeared mundane under magical scrutiny; whatever rested beneath the covers registered less like a spell than a reverberation of something ancient.

The party divided into a relay. Nobu took the first cart, Maeva and another watcher kept the second, Maki followed the third, and Clay held the fourth. Their earpieces kept the scattered pursuit connected as the carts crossed Bardune, passed through the river gate, and separated along flood-damaged roads.

For a while, the plan worked perfectly.

### One Bad Step

The third route narrowed into a muddy jungle road. Maki slipped, and the noise carried through the trees. Guards turned toward the movement. An attempt to hide the mistake by transforming Maki into an ordinary crocodile only gave them something more impossible to explain: a humanoid silhouette collapsing into a reptile directly in their line of sight.

The quiet pursuit became an improvised confidence trick. One party member assumed the appearance of the elderly pink-petaled Hanamori from Maki's vision—quickly dubbed **Sir Petalborn**—and stepped forward as though the entire encounter were authorized. The two guards, Chris and Jon, were not convinced. Arrows came out. The false elder took shafts in both shoulders while commands, lies, invisibility, and magic piled on top of one another.

The party finally forced the scene back under control. Jon was put to sleep and dragged into the trees. Chris's memory was rewritten, erasing the confrontation and replacing it with a version he could accept. Jon was bound and gagged behind a tree, stripped of his uniform, and impersonated well enough that the false Jon could return to Chris and continue walking beside the cart.

That solution left the real Jon alive behind them.

The party chose not to leave that loose end. They returned to the tree and cut his throat. The first attempt was too shallow; Jon woke fully, bleeding and screaming into the gag. The second killed him. A pursuit that had begun with strict orders not to reveal the operation now left a murdered Rootglass guard concealed beside the road.

They reported the body's rough location over the earpieces, caught up to the wagon, and kept moving.

### Where the Roads Met

Around two in the morning, the jungle opened beside the muddy [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Hakuryū Valley\|Hakuryū]]. The flood line stood above their heads on the trunks. Driftwood lay caught in exposed roots, while new green shoots pushed through the wreckage below. Near the river, old circular stonework had been cleared and restored: a central basin, a landing at the water, and four prepared beds arranged around it.

All four carts arrived. The four Root Wardens had reached the convergence site.

Three Ebizo then emerged from the river. One carried a satchel encased in coral. Maeva, close enough to the guards and caretakers to hear their exchange, learned that the newcomers were the carriers of the **Heart**. The Heart and Anchors were now in place. The Accord Bell was expected at sunrise.

The party ended the night already threaded through the gathering: Maeva near the operation, one of their number moving in a dead guard's identity, and the others hidden around the flood-scarred clearing. They had found the Stillwater Array. Their next task was to wait until it became whole—and then steal it from everyone assembled to protect it.

## Decisions and Consequences

- Maki used Ryoku's Revelation on the courier schedule and learned that the Anchors are called **Root Wardens**.
- The party learned that the Root Wardens react to one another and must travel at least twelve minutes apart.
- The group paid 1,000 gp and sacrificed a small portion of each participant's lifespan to mend a fracture in time.
- The ritual reduced the party's paradox from 12 points to 9.
- The Quiet Fox ordered the party to steal the completed Stillwater Array by any means and escape with it.
- The party donated 100 gp to Rootglass Conservatory while conducting reconnaissance.
- Piko confirmed that Rootglass's eastern yard routinely sends out wagons carrying medicines and botanical supplies.
- The party successfully followed all four Anchor carts to their convergence site.
- Maki's fall exposed the third-cart pursuit and forced the party into direct contact with its guards.
- The party altered Chris's memory and infiltrated the escort by impersonating Jon.
- The party deliberately killed the bound and gagged Jon to prevent him from exposing them.
- Maeva confirmed that the Ebizo arriving by river carried the Array's Heart.

## Current Situation

- All four Root Wardens and the Heart are at the Stillwater convergence site beside the Hakuryū.
- The Accord Bell is expected to arrive at sunrise.
- The Array is not yet complete, and the party's orders require them to wait until it is.
- Maeva is positioned close to the guards and caretakers; the rest of the party is distributed around the clearing.
- One party member has infiltrated the escort in Jon's stolen clothing and identity.
- Chris's memory has been modified, but the deception may fail under scrutiny.
- Jon's body remains tied to a tree along the third cart's route.
- The party still carries 9 points of paradox.

## Memorable Table Moments

- The Kappa declared his innocence before anyone mentioned cheating, while Ayame took one look at the cups and left.
- “Sir Petalborn” attempted to turn a failed stealth check, a spontaneous crocodile, and two suspicious archers into authorized Conservatory business.
- The false elder continued negotiating with an arrow in each shoulder.
- Jon's attempted execution began with a percentile roll so poor that it produced a nick, a muffled scream, and the immediate need for a second attempt.
- The party's status report reduced the entire roadside disaster to: fell down, made noise, changed a memory, took a man's place, killed him on a tree.

## Open Threads

- What does the completed Stillwater Array do, and why does the Quiet Fox want it stolen intact?
- Who is bringing the Accord Bell, and what protection will arrive with it at sunrise?
- What are the Root Wardens beneath their coverings, and what happens when all four can finally reach one another?
- Can the party steal the Array without destroying a site now crowded with Hanamori caretakers, Rootglass guards, and Ebizo?
- How long will Chris's altered memory and Jon's impersonation survive?
- When will Jon's body be discovered, and what will his murder cost the party?
- What will happen if the party's remaining paradox breaks loose before the operation is over?
- How much of their natural lives are they willing to trade to repair further damage to time?
