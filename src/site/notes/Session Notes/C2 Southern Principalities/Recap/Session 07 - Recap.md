# Southern Principalities — Session Record

## Session 7 Recap — The River Remembers

Nobu lay dead upon the beach at Mirror Lake. With ordinary healing unable to restore him, Sho used Timewalk and returned alone to the shrine one hour earlier.

Sho investigated the surface shrine, learning how its bell called the creature that gathered offerings below. He left a warning in the altar's dust—*The fish in the water. Do not look at them*—then returned to his own time. The warning worked. The party never approached the lake, and Sho returned to find Nobu alive beside their newly made campfire.

The rewritten night did not remain quiet. A second moon opened at the waterline and a towering shrine-bearer stepped through. It remembered a version of the Farmer's Shrine that the party had erased: a successful defence, an ambush, and a life left without purpose. Sho denied remembering the theft, while Maeva recognized the creature Clay had seen during their escape from the monastery. When it declared that stopping them had become its new purpose, the party chose to silence it rather than expose their operation.

The fight sprawled across the beach. Clay opened with a devastating poisoned shot, Sho knocked over the cracked bell and disrupted the shrine gathered around it, and the party endured warped gravity, binding cloth, folded paths, and repeated temporal displacements. Two Komainu emerged from the cedars and joined the attack. Nobu was battered and bound, Sho was knocked unconscious and carried in one lion's jaws, and the wagon—still holding the stolen offerings—began sliding toward the rising lake.

Before the fight could finish, the Dorotabō climbed onto the distant platform and emptied the Mirror Shrine's entire collection across its altar. Then the lake erupted. Bakuryō rose around the shrine: a river dragon vast enough to coil across the water, rest one claw upon the roof, and bring both Komainu and the shrine-bearer to the ground in submission.

Bakuryō dismissed the shrine-bearer, then offered the party one chance to return everything and have their trespass forgotten. They would neither surrender the offerings nor name the one who hired them, holding to their oath even after she made clear what the Hakuryū sustained and what its loss could cost the valley. She also recognized Sho and Maeva as one life divided into two courses and warned them that time remembers the lives they rewrite.

A colourless firework rose beyond the northern ridge. Beside it stood a broad silhouette carrying a burning flare and an enormous glaive. Bakuryō recognized the figure, tore free of the lake, and pursued it beyond the mountain.

The party used the opening. They rushed the damaged shrine, gathered its exposed offerings, and used Anomaly to tear one of the reflective koi from the lake. They fled Mirror Lake with the complete haul.

On the road home, the party stopped at the dead drop and began unloading the collected satchels. After the third was placed, a note appeared with new instructions: *Keep all the offerings. That is payment for the job.* They reclaimed the satchels and reached the Shigure Compound at approximately 3:00 a.m., where they finally rested.

## Decisions and Consequences

- Sho rewrote the previous night, preventing Nobu's death.
- Sho left information for his earlier self and returned to a changed present.
- The party attacked the shrine-bearer rather than acknowledge the erased theft.
- The party refused to identify its employer to Bakuryō.
- The party declined Bakuryō's final offer to return the stolen offerings.
- All four shrine collections remain in the party's possession as payment for the operation.
- The party removed one Mirror Koi from the lake.
- Each party member received the remaining **2,600 gp** promised for the job.
- The party returned safely to the Shigure Compound and completed a Long Rest.

## Current Situation

- All five party members are alive at the Shigure Compound.
- The party has four weeks of downtime ahead.
- The collected offerings and the Mirror Koi still require sorting and a decision about their use.
- The mission's consumables and magic items were recovered, but their final division was not completed at the table.
