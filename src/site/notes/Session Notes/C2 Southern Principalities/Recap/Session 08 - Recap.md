# Southern Principalities — Session Record

## Session 8 Recap — Four Weeks of Floodwater

The party returned from Mirror Lake at three in the morning and disappeared into the Shigure Compound for several days of hard-earned rest. Once everyone had recovered, they divided the operation's gold, consumables, and magic items, attuned to their new equipment, and stored the skin and scales taken from the Mirror Koi.

Then the [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Hakuryū Valley\|Hakuryū]] began to run backward.

At first, the change reached the compound as an impossible silence. Its stream stopped after dawn, trembled against its stones, and began flowing north. Ayame placed another offering at the family shrine. That evening, a soaked paper fox arrived with the first report: the Hakuryū had reversed, tributaries were tearing free of their channels, and the lower roads were closing.

Beyond the hidden valley, the disaster grew by the day. Waterwheels and irrigation wheels turned the wrong way. Boats pulled against their moorings. Fish gathered in drainage ditches, the water filled with churned mud, and roads became causeways and then islands. Farmers cut their own fields open to save homes uphill. Boats carried children, elders, seed grain, and ancestral tablets toward higher ground. [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Bardune/Bardune\|Bardune]] and [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/In'nali\|In'nali]] organized separate evacuations while the Shigure household quietly changed its trail markers and closed an approach before the flood could expose it.

The party knew which night the disaster began and what operation had drawn Bakuryō from the lake. They still did not know who turned the scale beneath her jaw, and the world around them did not stop long enough to demand an answer.

### Maeva and Sho Build a Future on a Crime

Maeva and Sho devoted all four weeks to an elaborate enterprise disguised as a fighting tournament. They researched locations and prospective marks, courted useful contacts, and settled upon [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Terran Rouselle\|Terran Rouselle]], a desperate warehouse owner burdened by gambling losses. After winning the [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Unnamed Ridge Warehouse\|property]] from him at poker, they left him in place as its manager and financier-facing public owner: the first layer between themselves and anything investigators might eventually uncover.

They made the empty, decrepit 30-by-60-foot warehouse temporarily serviceable, secured permits, spread flyers throughout the Southern Principalities, hired guards, and assembled a legitimate-looking event around a deeply illegitimate purpose. The plan grew into a five-day festival with twelve registered fighters, public challenge bouts, concessions, entry fees, and a gambling operation. The temporary setup proved the property's potential; permanently rebuilding it as a second archive, guild hall, and source of income remains a future project.

Maeva spent one of her carousing favours establishing [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Baron Husk\|Baron Husk]], a boastful self-proclaimed baron from In'nali who brought wealthy attention, an entourage, and a fighter to the tournament. Sho established [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Bughav\|Bughav]], a contact from [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Dothmeir\|Dothmeir]] who can locate special resources and uncommon items when given enough time.

Their timing was grimly perfect. By the fourth week, hundreds of exhausted and displaced people were desperate for a few days that were not about ruined homes and drowned fields. Survivors, merchants, fishing families, adventurers, and wealthy entourages converged upon the tournament. The house filled, wagers moved, businesses reportedly changed hands, and the new operation earned **980 gp** before its remaining accounts were settled.

The tournament's most chaotic attraction was an open brawl for a desperately useful prize: Dust of Dryness. One hundred entrants crowded into the ring. Maeva entered under a disguise, survived the improvised brackets, and emerged with the prize while Sho helped turn the confusion into a spectacle. Their intended final con—spreading rumours about a rigged favourite and forcing the real favourite to throw the decisive fight—remained the clearest expression of the business model they were building: legal enough to attract a crowd, crooked enough to ensure the house won.

### Nobu Searches the Past

Nobu returned to the Shigure archives looking for the mission that had poisoned his father. There was no record to find. [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Daichi Shigure\|Daichi]] had returned already consumed by the illness and never wrote the report, believing he would defeat it before normal duties mattered again.

[[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Ayame Shigure\|Ayame]] saw through Nobu's indirect questions almost immediately. Over dinner, she told him what his parents had managed to learn. They identified the poison's family but not its exact strain, and they heard of a possible cure they could never locate: a living plant of translucent glass, complete with crystalline roots, a five-inch stem, and a flower at its crown. Nobu now carries three pieces of research he can call upon as he searches for the plant, the poison's origin, and the place where Daichi was exposed.

During the following week, Nobu returned to the [[Compendium/Party/Quests/The Four Shrines\|Shrine of the Rapids]]. The riverbed was dry, the shrine warden was gone, and only an old bloodstain remained. New offerings had accumulated from people trying to appease [[Compendium/Lore/Deities/Kaiju/Bakuryō\|Bakuryō]], but the altar was still unbalanced from the party's theft. Nobu carefully restored its balance and left **67 gp** from the shrine job as an offering.

The shrine answered in a quieter way. As Nobu crossed its worn bridge, a sudden pain drove him to the railing and a vision opened behind his eyes: a tall natural pillar rising from a glass floor while lightning repeatedly struck it. Whether the place is connected to the glass flower, his father's poison, or something else remains unknown.

Nobu also learned to work his cards into his deceptions—not as literal magic, but as a practiced distraction that can create an opening when a conversation turns dangerous. He arrived at the tournament disguised as a staggering drunk and put the technique, if not his dignity, on display.

### Clay Returns to Ashura

Clay travelled alone to the [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Ashura Compound\|Ashura Compound]], devoted to the fallen demon god [[Compendium/Lore/Deities/Ashura\|Ashura]], donated to its shrine, and worked beside its smiths to make his cannon more destructive. The reforged weapon became a **+1 weapon**, difficult to disarm, instinctively aware of north and its elevation, and especially destructive against celestials.

Clay then searched the compound for memories of Ashura and of his own forgotten beginning. Instead, he found that the others carried the same emptiness. They remembered childhoods and families only in broad, interchangeable shapes, with no details sturdy enough to prove that any of those lives had happened. Clay's best answer was also the most unsettling one: perhaps beings like him simply live long enough to forget the people they were.

As Bakuryō's rampage worsened, would-be champions began passing through Ashura's compound. The first marched out alone, struck Bakuryō once on the nose with his maul, and was swallowed whole. A better-prepared adventuring company followed with boats, healers, historians, and supplies for stranded villages. Clay joined them, helped limit their losses during a failed first attempt, and learned the truth behind Bakuryō's fury.

The chevron-shaped guardian scale beneath the river dragon's jaw had been turned out of place. Bakuryō was the force reversing the rivers, but she was trapped in the rage caused by that displaced scale rather than acting from her ordinary will. During the company's second attempt, they endured long enough to turn it back. The fury left Bakuryō immediately. She lowered her head to the survivors, and the Hakuryū paused before finally beginning to flow downhill again.

Clay earned a favour from the victorious [[Compendium/Lore/Organizations/The Golden Company\|Golden Company]] for his aid and the respect of its [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Captain of the Golden Company\|captain]].

### Maki Finds His Way Through the Principalities

While the others buried themselves in archives, shrines, and elaborate crimes, Maki spent his first two weeks among the people who kept the Southern Principalities moving. He bought drinks and traded stories through taverns, dock offices, pilots' lounges, and merchant houses until two working relationships became something dependable.

In In'nali, he befriended **[[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Toma Cloudwake\|Toma Cloudwake]]**, a sharp-tongued Karasu Tengu who captains the independent cargo airship *Silver Cicada* from the Lower Lift Docks. Toma owes Maki **three favours**, potentially including discreet passage, quiet transportation for a person or modest cargo, or an introduction within In'nali's pilot community.

In Bardune, Maki earned the trust of **[[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Captain Nami Reedwake\|Captain Nami Reedwake]]**, the Huling captain of the shallow-draft merchant vessel *Quiet Heron*. Nami works between the Mosswater Quays and the smaller river and lakeside settlements, remembers every debt, and knows most of Bardune's working captains. She likewise owes Maki **three favours** involving passage, discreet transport, waterfront information, or introductions within the shipping community.

For the next two weeks, Maki left the respectable drinking houses behind and worked the markets, alleys, shrine steps, night stalls, and crowded streets beneath In'nali's Great Lift. There he caught **[[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Piko\|Piko]]**, a young Tanuki yokai, trying to pick his pocket.

Instead of turning Piko over or driving him away, Maki bought him food and listened. The boy proved to be an enthusiastic carrier of messages, collector of dubious "evidence," finder of shortcuts, and guide to places where adults had stopped noticing children. More importantly, Piko genuinely liked him. By the end of their time together, the young Tanuki regarded Maki as equal parts friend, older brother, and invitation to make extremely interesting decisions.

Piko knows In'nali's lower streets, can find people who do not want to be found, and hears what adults carelessly say around children. He is also willing to follow Maki somewhere dangerous if nobody stops him—which may be as much a complication as an advantage.

### What the River Left Behind

Bakuryō was restored, but restoration was not the same as repair. Floodwater remained across the valley. Homes and fields were destroyed, families displaced, and people killed trying to stop the dragon. Rice terraces lay under mud and debris. Drowned animals and stranded fish fouled the retreating water. Roads and trade routes would take months or years to rebuild, and shortages of rice and fish would reach far beyond the valley.

Bardune and In'nali had each protected their own people rather than cooperate. The shrine wardens still knew that someone had robbed all four shrines. The party still possessed the wealth taken from them.

Yet the month also left something new. Maeva and Sho had a foothold disguised as a business. Nobu had the first real shape of the cure he had been seeking. Clay had strengthened his weapon, uncovered another mystery about his origins, and earned allies beyond the Lotus Choir. Maki had quietly built routes into the air, across the water, and through the overlooked streets beneath In'nali.

The party's actions had helped break the valley. Their downtime showed what each of them intended to build in the space left behind.

## Decisions and Consequences

- The party divided the Mirror Lake operation's gold, consumables, and magic items.
- Maeva and Sho acquired the [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Unnamed Ridge Warehouse\|Unnamed Ridge Warehouse]] and used it as a temporary fighting venue; permanent expansion remains a future project.
- They retained the indebted former owner, [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Terran Rouselle\|Terran Rouselle]], as the operation's public-facing manager.
- Their first tournament drew hundreds of attendees and returned 980 gp before final accounting.
- Maeva won the open brawl and its prize, Dust of Dryness.
- Nobu learned that a glass-like flowering plant may cure Daichi's poison.
- Nobu holds three future research opportunities connected to the poison and its cure.
- Nobu restored the altar at the [[Compendium/Party/Quests/The Four Shrines\|Shrine of the Rapids]] and left 67 gp as an offering.
- Clay's cannon became a +1 weapon with additional unusual properties.
- Clay helped [[Compendium/Lore/Organizations/The Golden Company\|The Golden Company]] restore [[Compendium/Lore/Deities/Kaiju/Bakuryō\|Bakuryō]] and earned one favour from them.
- Maeva established [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Baron Husk\|Baron Husk]] as a continuing social contact.
- Sho established [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Bughav\|Bughav]] as a continuing procurement contact.
- Maki earned three favours each from Toma Cloudwake and Captain Nami Reedwake.
- Maki befriended Piko rather than punishing him for attempting to pick his pocket.
- The Hakuryū is flowing in its proper direction again.

## Current Situation

- The river reversal has ended, but the Southern Principalities face years of recovery.
- The party remains in possession of the offerings stolen from all four shrines.
- Maeva and Sho control an unnamed, decrepit warehouse whose intended legitimate and criminal futures remain undeveloped.
- Daichi's cure may depend upon locating a crystalline flower of unknown origin.
- Nobu's vision of a lightning-struck pillar above glass remains unexplained.
- Clay has found no reliable account of his life before his present existence.
- [[Compendium/Lore/Organizations/The Golden Company\|The Golden Company]] owes Clay a favour.
- Toma Cloudwake and Captain Nami Reedwake give Maki discreet transportation contacts in both In'nali and Bardune.
- Piko has become personally attached to Maki and may accompany him into danger unless someone intervenes.

## Memorable Table Moments

- Clay tried to turn donations to Ashura into a `1-800-RIVER` relief campaign while the valley flooded.
- The tournament gave Clay the stage name **the Porcelain Puppet**, whether he wanted it or not.
- Nobu's newly flashy weapon earned him five gold in tips, several dropped flourishes, and at least one lightly injured spectator.
- A one-hundred-person brawl for Dust of Dryness became the valley's most complicated form of disaster relief.
- The party spent nearly as much time making its crime administratively legitimate as it did planning the crime.
