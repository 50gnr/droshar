### Maki — Four Weeks of Downtime

Maki spent the first two weeks moving through the taverns, dock offices, pilots' lounges, and merchant houses used by the Southern Principalities' working middle class. Drinks were bought, stories were traded, and Maki made himself useful often enough that two relationships stuck.

**[[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Toma Cloudwake\|Toma Cloudwake]]** — Airship Pilot, In'nali  
A lean Karasu Tengu in his early forties with smoke-grey feathers, a chipped brass beak-ring, and a permanently wind-tossed blue flight coat. Toma captains the *Silver Cicada*, a small independent cargo airship operating out of **In'nali's Lower Lift Docks**. He is competent, sarcastic, and much more interested in getting paid and staying airborne than politics.

Maki earned **three favors from Toma**. Within reason, these could include discreet passage aboard the *Silver Cicada*, moving a person or modest cargo without asking many questions, or getting information/introduction within In'nali's pilot community.

**[[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Captain Nami Reedwake\|Captain Nami Reedwake]]** — Ship Captain, Bardune  
A broad-shouldered Huling woman with russet fur, several silver hoops along one ear, and heavy green river leathers that always smell faintly of cedar and lakewater. Nami commands the *Quiet Heron*, a shallow-draft merchant vessel that runs passengers and cargo between **Bardune's Mosswater Quays** and the smaller settlements around the lake and river system. She laughs loudly, remembers debts forever, and knows nearly every working captain on Bardune's waterfront.

Maki earned **three favors from Nami**. These could reasonably cover passage, discreet transport, waterfront information, or an introduction to someone in Bardune's shipping community.

---

For his next two weeks, Maki went considerably lower.

Rather than spending money in respectable drinking houses, he worked the markets, alleys, shrine steps, night stalls, and crowded streets beneath **In'nali's Great Lift**, looking for exactly the sort of kid most adults stopped noticing.

He found **[[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Piko\|Piko]]**.

Piko is a young **Tanuki yokai**, perhaps eleven or twelve, although he refuses to give Maki a straight answer. He's barely four feet tall, with sandy-brown fur, dark mask markings around enormous amber eyes, an oversized patched traveller's coat, and a battered little satchel containing an astonishing collection of stolen maps, bent keys, chalk, string, snacks, and things he insists are "important evidence."

Piko survives by carrying messages, finding shortcuts, guiding strangers, scavenging places he shouldn't be, and occasionally acquiring things whose owners were apparently "finished with them."

Their friendship started when Maki caught him trying to pick his pocket.

Instead of turning him over or chasing him off, Maki bought him food and spent the afternoon listening while Piko enthusiastically explained several things about In'nali that Maki supposedly "didn't understand."

With an excellent result—and an absurdly lucky first meeting—Piko didn't become merely another street contact.

**He likes Maki.**

Piko now treats Maki somewhere between a friend, an older brother, and a source of extremely interesting bad decisions. He knows the lower streets of In'nali exceptionally well, can find people who don't want to be found, hears plenty of things adults say around children, and is absolutely willing to accompany Maki somewhere dangerous if nobody stops him.

Which may eventually become a problem.
