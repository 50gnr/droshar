# Southern Principalities — Session Record

## Session 2 Recap — The Rapids Shrine

The party left the Shigure Compound beneath heavy clouds, heading east toward their first shrine operation. At the Hakuryū crossing, they found the nightly Lantern Crossing underway: the bridge closed, lanterns drifting beneath its arches, and travelers waiting in respectful silence. Rather than disturb the ritual, the party turned west toward the Shrine of the Rapids.

The western road proved considerably less peaceful. Four kamaitachi attacked from the storm-darkened forest, landing several vicious blows before the party drove them off. Two escaped into the trees, but nobody felt compelled to chase razor-clawed wind monsters through a thunderstorm.

At the shrine, Clay established overwatch while Maki remained with the wagon. Nobu moved ahead to scout and immediately nearly fell into the raging river after a disastrous attempt to swing beneath one of the bridges. Maki caught him with **Bigby’s Hand**, preserving both the operation and whatever dignity Nobu had left.

Nobu continued reconnaissance using the **Earrings of Whisper** while Maeva and Sho approached invisibly. He engaged Genpei, the elderly kappa caretaker, in an increasingly circular conversation that included an unfortunately timed bathroom break. When subtlety finally ran out, Nobu put Genpei to sleep for one minute.

Maeva, Sho, and Nobu grabbed the four offering bundles and ran.

Then the river answered.

The session ended as a massive wave crashed through the shrine, with Nobu and Sho carrying one bundle each, Maeva carrying one with the fourth magnetized firmly to her backside, Clay still 150 feet away on overwatch, Maki beside the wagon, and Genpei asleep behind them.

A clean professional operation, more or less.

---

## Decisions and Consequences

- The party respected the Lantern Crossing and changed routes.
    
- Two kamaitachi were defeated; two escaped.
    
- Nobu conducted reconnaissance and spoke directly with Genpei.
    
- Maeva and Sho entered the shrine invisibly.
    
- Genpei was put to sleep rather than harmed.
    
- All four ceremonial offering bundles were removed.
    
- The river reacted immediately when the offerings left the altar.
    
- Genpei may be able to identify Nobu once he awakens.
