# Southern Principalities - Session Record

> The party prepares for shrine investigations, develops infiltration strategies, and plans logistics for their journey through the Southern Principalities.

## Session Recap

The players received a briefing regarding investigating four shrines in Hakuryū Valley while maintaining secrecy. They discussed accessing an archive related to Nobu's father and began planning retrieval strategies for offerings, categorizing shrines by difficulty and considering Maki’s involvement. The Farmer's Shrine was identified as a potential infiltration target, with plans to trace offerings and potentially use a trance ability.  The party is currently located at the Shigure Compound and will travel approximately one day between shrines. They are also preparing for their journey, securing items in hidden compartments within a cart, disguising political markings, and distributing rewards including Jade carvings and healing charms. Clay has been assigned as the driver and lookout for the cart.

## Major Beats

### Briefing and Archive Access 

The players received their mission briefing to investigate shrines in Hakuryū Valley, emphasizing secrecy. Discussions centered on accessing an archive related to Nobu's father and planning for offering retrieval.

### Farmer's Shrine Infiltration Planning (00:15:02)

The players devised a plan to infiltrate the Farmer’s Shrine, considering quick entry and exit while minimizing interaction with the caretaker. They intend to trace offerings and potentially use a trance ability.

### Logistical Preparations & Travel Planning (01:44:55)

The party focused on logistical preparations for the mission, including securing items in a cart, disguising political markings, and planning routes. Clay will drive and act as lookout.

- The players are tasked with investigating four shrines in Hakuryū Valley.
- The mission requires maintaining secrecy and avoiding attention.
- The party is currently located at the Shigure Compound.
- Travel time between shrines is approximately one day.
- The Farmer’s Shrine is family-maintained and open daily.
- The shrine has few obvious security concerns.
- Locations have multiple access points, stairways, and towers.
- Shrines are dedicated to kaiju, river gods, and mountain spirits.
- Offerings disappear mysteriously at shrines.
- A bell/gong alerts people to approaching offerers.
- Mirror Lake Shrine assumption is that it contains a creature.
- Each Jade carving is worth 250 GP.
- A healing charm provides a healing effect equivalent to 2d4 + 4.
- The cart has a side door, front bench, and steering wheel.
