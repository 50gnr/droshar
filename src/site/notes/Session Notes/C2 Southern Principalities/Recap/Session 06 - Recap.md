# Southern Principalities — Session Record

## The Shrine Break

With their deception exposed and the Avatar calling Maeva out, the party committed to the escape.

A Shatter bomb tore a ten-foot opening through the rear of the shrine. Door, walls, roof, and floor gave way once  Clay and Nobu had kicked all five packages into open air beneath their parachutes.

Clay and Nobu drank Potions of Flying and followed the falling cargo. Maeva used Dimension Door to place herself high in the open sky, then Polymorphed into a giant eagle during the fall. Sho was also Polymorphed into a giant eagle, carrying Maki back to the wagon.

Maki immediately drove west, trying to hide the cart and draw as little attention as possible from the Avatar and the monks.

## Five Packages Down

Every package reached the ground.

Clay shepherded four of them, though two struck the ground and had to be recovered. Once down, he fashioned carrying bags for his shifting Haniwa companion—then in the form of a mule—and loaded all four onto it.

Nobu and Sho handled the fifth package. Once clear of pursuit, they emptied its offerings into Sho’s Hat of Holding.

During Maeva and Clay’s escape, a temporal rift opened and an unfamiliar figure stepped through. It raised a shrine of its own. Maeva answered with Mislead, and she and Clay escaped.

By roughly 9:00 a.m., the party and all five packages were off the mountain. They split up, evaded pursuit, and eventually brought the recovered offerings back to the cart.

## Twelve Hours Later

At approximately 9:00 p.m., the party converged on the Mirror Shrine.

Nobu looked into the water and fell under the influence of the Mirror Koi. He walked into the lake and continued down to the submerged shrine, where silt and weeds held him among the offerings. Unable to free himself, Nobu suffocated, drowned, and did not survive.

Sho and Maki went in after him. They recovered Nobu’s body and returned to shore, brushing past the Dorotabō with escape as their only concern.

## Where We Left Them

The session ended at approximately 10:00 p.m.

- Nobu’s body lies on the beach beside Sho and Maki.
- Clay and Maeva are in or near the cart.
- All five packages from the monastery were recovered.
- The Mirror Shrine remains below the lake.
- The party must decide whether—and how—to bring Nobu back.

## Open Threads

- Nobu is dead, but death may not be the end of the decision.
- The Mirror Koi and the submerged shrine remain unresolved.
- The Dorotabō is still nearby.
- An unknown figure emerged from a temporal rift during the escape.
- The consequences of destroying the monastery shrine have yet to arrive.

> The mountain was escaped. The lake collected its offering.
