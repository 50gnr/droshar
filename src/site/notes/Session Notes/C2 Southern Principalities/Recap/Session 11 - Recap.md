# Southern Principalities — Session Record

## Session 11 Recap — Take Stillwater

The four [[Root Wardens]] stood in separate beds around an old stone basin beside the [[Hakuryū Valley|Hakuryū]]. Three Ebizo guarded the coral-cased Heart. The [[Accord Bell]] had yet to arrive. The party had followed the components here under orders from the [[Quiet Fox]]: let the [[Stillwater Array]] become whole, then take it.

Before sunrise, a small moss-covered yokai slipped through the hiding places and delivered the next instruction on a scrap of parchment. Once the Array was secured, the party was to cross the Hakuryū to the east and follow a marked route to an abandoned warehouse. Further instructions would wait there. The message gave them a destination. It did not give them a way to carry four enormous, living Wardens across a fast river.

### The Bell and the Flower

The missing guardian arrived in deep green: an Oniborne bearing the bronze Bell on a carrying frame. With him came an assembly lead in a travel-worn coat. A small crystalline flower was pinned to it. Nobu watched the flower closely, wondering whether it might connect to the glass-bloom cure he sought for his father. From that distance, he could not tell. The man’s identity—and what the flower meant—remained unknown.

Around the clearing, the work continued with deliberate care. One Root Warden’s fingers began to turn toward its neighbour. Its caretaker pressed both hands into the soil until the movement ceased. Drivers repositioned their oxen and carts. Guards shifted posts. The Ebizo elder kept the Heart secured until the moment it was needed, and the Bell’s clapper was bound so it would not sound by accident. Every precaution suggested the same thing: these pieces mattered, and bringing them together carried a risk no one wanted to test casually.

Then they tested it anyway.

The caretakers brought the Wardens into alignment. Roots linked the four stations and a green-gold boundary rose around the basin. The Ebizo elder set the Heart in place; clear water welled up. A pale, koi-like ribbon of spirit tried to rush toward the river. The Oniborne rang the Accord Bell, and the light became a net. It caught the fleeing presence and guided it back to the basin.

“It worked!” a driver cried.

For one brief moment, relief crossed the clearing. The party had seen the completed Array do something real: contain and return a spirit drawn toward the river. They had **not** learned that it could hold every spirit, or the one they feared most. The assembly lead ordered the measurements recorded and the components returned to their separate custodians. Nothing was to be left together longer than necessary. The Bell sounded again, the net fell away, and the dismantling began.

That was the opening the party had waited for.

### Heart First

The first move was quiet in exactly one place. A silence bomb burst over the Bell, cutting off its sound as a portal opened in the middle of the Array. Maki stepped through, lifted the coral Heart from the basin, and vanished across the Hakuryū with it.

The clearing did not stay quiet. The Ebizo reacted at once, launching their boat into the hard current after the stolen Heart. The other custodians shouted, and the Oniborne strapped the Bell to his back. The party had removed one indispensable piece, but had also turned a carefully managed handover into an alarm heard across the whole site.

The fight that followed came apart in several directions. A shot knocked the Oniborne down before he could carry the Bell away. One party member, still wearing the dead guard Jon’s identity, tried to curse him; the magic failed. The guardian answered with darkness and then rose into the air, Bell still on his back. Near the carts, guards and Hanamori caretakers tried to free the Wardens’ roots and load them before the attackers could reach the remaining components.

Clay made a choice with consequences beyond the immediate fight. He fired on the assembly lead—the man with the crystalline flower—and the shot tore him in two. Nobu had wanted to know who he was and what he carried. A party member recovered the lead’s measurement book from beside the body, but its owner could no longer answer a question. The flower’s possible connection to Nobu’s father was left more urgent and less certain than before.

The party hit the transport operation as hard as the defenders hit back. A hypnotic-pattern bomb hung a kaleidoscope of light over one cart and held its guards staring upward. Clay shot through guards and oxen, destroying the teams that might have hauled the Wardens away. Chris—the guard whose memory had been altered on the road the previous night—found the fight and turned on the attackers. The caretakers used their own strange defenses, including crushing gravity and spores that left Nobu increasingly exposed.

Across the chaos, the Oniborne remained the most dangerous obstacle to taking the Bell. Maeva transformed Sho into a huge flying dinosaur so they could intercept him from above. The plan produced a startling image over the clearing; it did not settle the fight. Maeva later dropped back to the ground under the guardian’s blows, and the struggle over the Bell continued at close range while the river and the Wardens remained separate problems.

### The Gate Closes

The party finally found a way to separate the Bell from its carrier. Acid ate through the fastening on the Oniborne’s frame. As the Bell fell, Sho opened an **Arcane Gate** beneath it. The bronze weight dropped through to the far bank, and Sho followed. Maeva escaped through the same gate moments later. Sho closed it behind them before the Oniborne could use the opening.

They had the Heart. They had the Bell. They did **not** have the four Root Wardens.

Nor had everyone crossed. Nobu was stunned and bleeding on the original bank. A blow would have dropped him unconscious, but a time-bending intervention kept him standing at **one hit point**. The gate’s closure left him there with Clay while the others were across the river or farther into the brush. Nobu was alive, but in no condition to carry himself out of the clearing.

Clay stayed near him and fought through the remaining guards and shrine bearers. Some tried to run; the last of them did not get away. By the end of the fighting, the site’s defenders were dead apart from the Oniborne and the Ebizo, both now on the far side of the Hakuryū. The Oniborne had lost the Bell but not the will—or the ability—to pursue it.

The party began sketching a rescue: use their remaining teleportation to return for Nobu, move him out of sight, and then deal with the Wardens. They also talked through the far harder problem of transporting four eight-foot, root-bound components to the warehouse without letting them reach for one another. Those were plans, not completed escapes. The session ended with the theft only partly accomplished and the clearing no longer a place anyone could mistake for a careful handover.

## Decisions and Consequences

- The completed Array was tested. Its Wardens, Heart, and Bell formed a luminous net that caught a pale spirit trying to reach the river and returned it to the basin.
- The Quiet Fox’s new message directed the party across the Hakuryū to an abandoned warehouse after securing the Array.
- Maki stole the Heart by portal and carried it across the river; the three Ebizo gave chase.
- Clay killed the assembly lead who wore the crystalline flower. A party member recovered his measurement book, but the lead’s identity and the flower’s significance remain unresolved.
- The party disabled the Bell’s carrier, and Sho used an Arcane Gate to move the Bell to the far bank. Maeva followed him through.
- Clay cleared the remaining defenders while guarding the badly wounded Nobu. The Oniborne and Ebizo survived and remain loose beyond the river.
- The Root Wardens were **not** transported. The party still needs a way to move all four.

## Current Situation

- The **Heart** is across the Hakuryū with Maki; the **Accord Bell** has also reached the far bank with Sho and Maeva.
- All **four Root Wardens** remain at the bloodied assembly site on the original bank. The Array worked, but it is no longer assembled.
- **Nobu is alive**, stunned and at **one hit point**, with Clay near him on the original bank. Getting him safely away is the immediate priority.
- The **Oniborne** survived the fight and crossed the river without the Bell. The **Ebizo** also crossed after Maki and the Heart. Neither threat has been resolved.
- The party has the assembly lead’s measurement book and a route to the Quiet Fox’s warehouse, but has not delivered the Array there.

## Memorable Table Moments

- A Warden reached toward its neighbour so slowly that only its turning fingers betrayed it; a caretaker calmed it with two hands in the soil.
- The successful test drew an honest cheer of “It worked!” just before the entire operation became a robbery.
- A kaleidoscopic spell bomb stopped a cart’s guards mid-crisis, while the party argued about how to move four living trees across a raging river.
- Maeva’s answer to a flying Oniborne was to make Sho a flying dinosaur. The fight still came down to acid, a falling Bell, and a very well-placed gate.
- With the Bell finally across, the celebration lasted only long enough for someone to ask how they were going to save Nobu.

## Open Threads

- How will the party rescue Nobu and move all four Root Wardens across the Hakuryū without reassembling the Array in the wrong place?
- Can Maki stay ahead of the Ebizo with the Heart, and can Sho and Maeva keep the Bell from the surviving Oniborne?
- What did the assembly lead record in his book, and why was he wearing a crystalline flower that caught Nobu’s attention?
- What exactly did the Array contain during its test—and what are the limits of that protection?
- What will the Quiet Fox say when the party reaches the warehouse with two components, four Wardens still stranded, and a clearing full of dead custodians?
