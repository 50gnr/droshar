# Southern Principalities — Session Record

## Session 15 Recap — After Hours

### Into a City Under Curfew

The HALO jump from **Toma's skyship** mostly went to plan. Nobu tumbled during the descent, but the party landed properly in a service courtyard. A little magical persuasion sent the porter back to his work, leaving them to find their way through In'nali. It was around ten at night, and the city had changed since their last visit. Shuttered shops lined the streets, patrols escorted night deliveries, and lanterns marked checkpoints where papers were being checked. One member traveled invisibly while the others tried to look as though they belonged outside after curfew.

The disguises became a performance of their own. A supposed guard escorted an elderly woman, while a companion walking ahead explained that this guard had already checked his papers. The delivery escort let him continue, with instructions to stop wandering away from his minder. At a larger checkpoint, two members bypassed the guards by Dimension Door. The disguised pair instead staged a search for the woman's papers in an alley, complete with outdated documents, flustered explanations, and an order to come along for replacements. They made it through. A slow clap from behind them suggested the performance had been appreciated, whether or not it had been believed.

The group reunited at Maki's shop, whose name acquired more M's with every attempt to say it. Behind the barred doors were shelves of colorful sweets, a preparation kitchen, Remy's loft office, and bedrolls in the rear stockroom. Remy had left a note: the staff had tomorrow off, and he would return in the morning. For the moment, the five of them had somewhere dry and private to settle in.

### The Best Customer in the Shop

Nobu tried the candy. Then he wanted another. Buying a few became sneaking a few, until he was thoroughly intoxicated and having difficulty thinking about anything beyond the next piece. Maki's explanation that sugar could be addictive did little to settle everyone else's questions. It did, however, leave room for a sale: **150 gold pieces for a lifetime candy arrangement**, with a little refill pouch to show the staff. Maeva eventually cast Greater Restoration on Nobu, clearing his head and the immediate effects. Sobriety brought an attempt to sell the arrangement back. Maki pointed to the all-sales-final policy. Nobu had recovered his judgment, but not his money, and more candy could put him back where he had started.

The quieter conversation left other matters unfinished. Nobu explained that his father's condition was connected to a crystal flower and that his earlier investigation had found no cure. The party also questioned the shop's security. Maki trusted its barred doors and the guards outside; no magical alarm protected it. Sho took the loft and worked on possibilities for the supper, while the others bedded down in the stockroom. Donating candy to the event—and perhaps hiding a shrunken Clay inside a jawbreaker—joined the ideas still awaiting practical investigation.

### The Night They Had to Undo

Scraping beneath the stockroom disturbed the sleepers. A mouse and a falling sack briefly confused the matter, but Clay sensed someone under the floorboards. Boards lifted, a lantern appeared, and an intruder climbed into a room already full of waiting witnesses. He claimed Remy had hired him to fix the pipes. The encounter deteriorated quickly. Clay hauled him onto the table and broke his knee. Another intruder escaped Nobu's grasp by throwing sugar into his eyes, then retreated down the tunnel. The wounded man was still dangerous: he fired an arcane pistol and struck with a dagger as he tried to get away.

Maeva answered with **Destructive Wave**. The blast killed the wounded intruder, hurled companions into walls, and blew those walls outward. The stockroom became a cloud of sugar, splintered furniture, and debris, with the party's ears ringing inside the wreckage. Whistles and approaching responders left little time to argue about who would rebuild Maki's investment. All five squeezed into the burglars' narrow tunnel and crawled away beneath the street. This was the disaster they would shortly erase.

The passage led into a cellar beneath **Amekado Sweets**, where Genzo Amekado and the remaining burglars had gathered. Clay caught them by surprise. His intended knockout with the odzutsu ended in a crushing death; a second strike killed another man, and the last fleeing burglar was cut down on the stairs. Maki seized Genzo with Bigby's Hand. The confectioner admitted that he wanted the secret recipe behind Maki's success. While Maeva and Sho withdrew to attempt a rewind, the retaliation continued: Genzo was killed, and his body became an instrument for leaving furious messages on his own walls. A covert arrival had become a ruined refuge and five deaths.

### A Second Version of the Evening

Maeva took a dead man's boot as evidence, brought Sho with her, and used **Timewalk to go back one hour**. They reached Genzo while his crew was still digging toward Maki's floor. They warned him what would happen if the burglary continued and showed him the boot as proof of the bloody future. Genzo called down the passage for Renga and ordered everyone back. Maeva and Sho waited until all four burglars had returned. The confrontation ended with tea and some of Maki's candy rather than another fight. The crew never broke through the stockroom floor, and the explosion, pursuit, and killings were undone. When the time travelers returned to their present, they used Dimension Door to get back to Maki's loft.

Morning light found the shop intact. Maki had dreamlike impressions of a much worse night; Maeva explained what had happened and why they had intervened. The conversation became a pointed question about priorities: did the shop matter more than the Lotus Choir and the operation they had come to carry out? Maki affirmed that the Choir came first. Remy then arrived with **papers for everyone**, giving them something more useful than another checkpoint improvisation. An Alarm spell or other security system was recommended, and Maki rewarded Remy with **150 gold pieces**. The shop was safe for the moment, its rivals were alive, and the unfinished tunnel still approached beneath it. Access to the supper and a way out of In'nali remained work for the days ahead.

## Decisions and Consequences

- The party landed in In'nali and reached Maki's shop without a fight with the patrols or checkpoint guards.
- Nobu paid **150 gold** for a lifetime candy arrangement. Maeva cleared his immediate condition with Greater Restoration; the candy remained a risk if he returned to it.
- The burglary exposed an underground approach to the shop and Genzo Amekado's interest in its recipe.
- The destroyed shop, four dead burglars, and dead Genzo belonged to the **superseded timeline**. Maeva and Sho's intervention prevented that outcome.
- Remy delivered papers for the whole party. Maki paid him **150 gold**, and the group recommended better security.
- Maki affirmed that the Lotus Choir took priority over the shop.

## Current Situation

The party was together at Maki's intact candy shop on the morning after their arrival. They had papers, and Remy was back at work. Genzo and all four burglars were alive; the tunnel had been abandoned before it broke through the stockroom floor, but the underground approach still existed. A magical alarm had been suggested, not installed. In'nali remained under curfew and tighter security, and the operation against Aketsu Blackwing still lay ahead.
