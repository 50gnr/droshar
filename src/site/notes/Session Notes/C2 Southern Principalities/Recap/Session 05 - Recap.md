# Session 05 — The Monastery of Falling Clouds

> The road ended beneath a thousand feet of sandstone.
> 
> What followed was a long climb through roots, waterfalls, broken ledges, exposed stone, and cloud.

## Session Recap

The party began the ascent toward the **Monastery of Falling Clouds**, working their way through five increasingly dangerous sections of the mountain.

It was not a graceful climb.

Bad footing, bad luck, and more than a few spectacular failures followed them upward, but ropes, climbing gear, magic, and teamwork kept everyone moving until the party finally reached the last shelf beneath the summit.

During the ascent, a **massive black-winged shadow** crossed the western mountains in the distance.

Maki attempted to scry on the creature but failed to gain a view of it.

Whatever it was continued west.

At the summit, the party found the monastery very much alive.

Monks moved through courtyards and walkways according to an established rhythm while the upper prayer hall overlooked the mountain from behind the main complex.

Rather than immediately entering, the group began reconnaissance.

Under Sho’s direction, Nobu worked his way around the exterior of the mountain and reached the rear of the prayer hall.

Maeva took another approach.

Assuming the form of a **Karasu Tengu**, she joined the monks moving through the monastery and began observing the shrine from within.

Between them, the party learned considerably more about the site:

- The prayer hall follows a regular monk rotation.
    
- The offerings are concentrated inside the upper shrine.
    
- The rear of the shrine provides alternate access.
    
- A lift and pulley system serves the back of the structure.
    
- The monastery’s routines create exploitable windows for movement.
    
- A mosaic and carving within the shrine continue to connect Bakuryō to the strange scale beneath her jaw.
    

Maeva and Nobu eventually returned to Sho with what they had learned, and planning resumed beneath the summit ledge.

Nobu was then sent back toward the rear of the shrine while Maeva went looking for monastery clothing that could disguise Sho and Maki.

That part of the operation went less smoothly.

Maeva approached the monastery’s lead uniform dyer and his two assistants, but her performance did not survive their scrutiny.

Rather than securing uniforms, she found herself being escorted deeper into the monastery.

Her destination:

**The main temple.**

Waiting there is the monastery’s Avatar.

## Current Position

- **Maeva** — Being escorted to the main temple by the uniform dyer and two assistants
    
- **Nobu** — Behind the upper prayer hall
    
- **Sho, Maki, and Clay** — Hidden beneath the summit ledge
    
- **Offerings** — Still inside the upper prayer hall
    
- **Monastery** — No general alarm has been raised
    

The operation remains intact.

For now.
