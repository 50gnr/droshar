# Southern Principalities — Session Record

## Session 4 Recap — The Shrine That Wouldn’t Stay Empty

The party reached the Farmer’s Shrine after a cautious afternoon of reconnaissance.

Clay spent the daylight hours watching the shrine, the family, and the surrounding fields, building a clear picture of the routine before the group finally committed to the operation after dark.

Maki took the first step.

Under the guise of distraction, she delivered a full-throated rendition of **“Let It Go,”** holding the farmer and his family completely captivated while the others moved.

Maeva and Nobu entered the shrine and began loading the offerings.

Inside, they discovered **Tsubu**.

The tiny unseen shrine keeper became the complication nobody had planned for. They tried to contain him while continuing the theft, but eventually lost track of him.

Tsubu reached the bell.

And rang it.

The operation unraveled.

Then Sho changed the answer.

He pulled himself one hour backward through time, carrying the knowledge of the failed attempt with him.

This time, he knew exactly where the weaknesses were.

Tsubu never reached the bell.

The offerings were packed cleanly.

Tsubu was left sleeping, his memory altered so that nobody had ever entered the shrine at all.

With the work complete, the party disappeared from the shrine in a flash of magic, reappearing at the wagon before continuing the exfiltration.

As far as anyone in the farming hamlet knows, nothing happened.

As far as the shrine remembers, that may be another question entirely.

The wagon now turns toward the **Monastery of Falling Clouds**.
