## At a Glance

Piko survives beneath In'nali's Great Lift by carrying messages, finding shortcuts, guiding strangers, scavenging forbidden places, and occasionally acquiring things whose owners were apparently “finished with them.”

His satchel holds stolen maps, bent keys, chalk, string, snacks, and whatever currently qualifies as important evidence.

## Running Piko

- **Voice:** Fast, confident, and delighted to explain what adults do not understand.
- **Wants:** Food, interesting secrets, and a place in whatever Maki is doing.
- **Useful For:** Lower-street routes, finding elusive people, overheard information, messages, and access to places a child is ignored.
- **Limit:** Piko will volunteer for danger he does not fully understand.

## Connection to Maki

Piko met [[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Maki\|Maki Maki]] while trying to pick his pocket. Maki bought him food instead of turning him over and listened to his advice about In'nali.

Piko now treats Maki as a friend, an older brother, and a reliable source of interesting bad decisions.
