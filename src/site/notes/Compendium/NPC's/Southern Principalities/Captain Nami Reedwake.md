## At a Glance

Nami commands the *Quiet Heron*, a shallow-draft merchant vessel carrying passengers and cargo between [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Bardune/Bardune\|Bardune's]] Mosswater Quays and smaller settlements throughout the lake and river system.

## Running Nami

- **Voice:** Big laugh, plain speech, no indoor volume.
- **Wants:** Honest terms, capable company, and debts remembered.
- **Useful For:** Passage, discreet transport, waterfront information, or introductions within Bardune's shipping community.
- **Limit:** A favor may bend a rule; it does not erase a debt or endanger her crew without cause.

## Connection to Maki

[[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Maki\|Maki Maki]] became a familiar and useful face among the captains and merchants of Bardune's waterfront.

**Favors owed: 3**
