# Bughav

## At a Glance

A broker from [[Dathmyr\|Dathmyr]] who finds special resources, uncommon items, and better prices for clients willing to wait. Bughav is rarely the person holding what a client needs. He is the person who knows who might—and who is willing to keep asking until the right seller appears.

## Running Bughav

- **Role:** Slow but effective procurement broker
- **Method:** Consults friends, compares sellers, and follows chains of introductions
- **Strength:** Eventually finds better terms than the obvious market
- **Cost:** Time and uncertainty
- **Useful For:** Special materials, uncommon items, obscure suppliers, and price negotiation
- **Limit:** He is not the contact for urgent procurement

## At the Table

- Never promises speed.
- Answers a request with the name of somebody else he needs to speak with.
- Treats waiting as part of getting a good price, not as a failure.
- Returns with an actual option once enough time has passed.
- Best used when the party can give him several days or longer.

## Connection to Sho

[[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Sho\|Sho]] established Bughav as a recurring procurement contact during Session 08. Bughav became Sho's preferred contact for unusual purchases that matter more than their delivery date.

Their connection is established, but Bughav does not currently owe Sho a numbered favour. Each request still requires time, money, and whatever terms the eventual seller demands.
