# Iori Sazanami

![Iori Sazanami.png](/img/user/Assets/Images/NPC/Iori%20Sazanami.png)

An older female karasu tengu who provides magical security and event consultation at the [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/High Circles Reception Hall\|High Circles Reception Hall]]. Grey feathers, a white crest, and chalk-marked cuffs; she listens carefully before deciding whether a proposed effect presents a problem.

- **Voice:** Patient, precise, practical. Happy to explain a service without turning the explanation into a lecture.
- **Wants:** Safe events, clear instructions, and declared magical effects she can assess properly.
- **Useful for:** Magical entertainment approvals, private-event screening, and the venue's security arrangements.
