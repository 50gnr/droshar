## At a Glance

The former owner and current manager of the [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Unnamed Ridge Warehouse\|Unnamed Ridge Warehouse]]. Terran was overwhelmed by debt and preparing to sell when he lost the property to [[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Maeva\|Maeva]] and [[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Sho\|Sho]] in a poker game.

They kept him in place because he knows the property and its business better than they do.

Terran has let himself go under the pressure, though he remains lean rather than heavyset. His most memorable feature is a sparse, poorly kept moustache. Behind his desk sits a scuffed leather ball tied to a rope; whenever stress begins to show, he kicks it against the wall and lets the tether drag it back.

## Running Terran

- **Role:** Manager and financier-facing public owner
- **Wants:** Keep the warehouse operating and recover from his debts
- **Useful For:** Local administration, permits, suppliers, and maintaining the property's legitimate face
- **Pressure Point:** Gambling and financial desperation
- **Current Loyalty:** Practical rather than proven

## Voice and Mannerisms

- Tries to sound like the man still in charge, then remembers who owns the building.
- Straightens permits, ledgers, or anything else within reach when nervous.
- Kicks the tethered ball behind his desk when under pressure.
- Knows the warehouse intimately and becomes defensive when anyone treats it as worthless.
- Exhausted rather than incompetent; given stability, he may prove useful.

## Current Arrangement

- Maeva and Sho control the property.
- Terran manages its ordinary operation.
- Outsiders and financiers may still treat him as the owner.
- He provides distance between the party and anything investigated at the site.
