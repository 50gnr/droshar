## At a Glance

The tanuki proprietor of [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/Amekado Sweets\|Amekado Sweets]], an established confectionery a few premises from [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/Maki's Candy Shop\|Maki's Candy Shop]] on In'nali's plateau. Genzo makes excellent sesame brittle, pulled sugar, and preserved-fruit sweets. He takes considerable pride in all three.

- **Appearance:** White whiskers, round brass spectacles, green apron; his thick tail brushes the legs of his stool.
- **Manner:** Meticulous, proprietorial, easily offended by criticism of his craft.
- **Voice:** Polite and precise; becomes markedly less precise when flustered.
- **Habit:** Straightens the sweets in a display while someone is speaking to him.

# Hidden Data

## The Rivalry

- Resents the attention [[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Maki\|Maki]] has drawn so quickly. His own sweets are good; he cannot reproduce the new shop's unusual effects.
- Suspects a concealed ingredient or acquired recipe. Knows nothing of its source, the Lotus Choir, or the assassination.
- Has convinced himself that stealing a sample is protecting his livelihood.
- Hired [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Renga\|Renga]] and three helpers to bring back recipe notes and ingredient samples. Paid **80 gp up front**, with **120 gp on delivery**.
- Authorized a break-in, not murder, arson, or kidnapping. That does not excuse the burglary.
- Let the crew reopen a disused passage from his cellar and breach the floor of Maki's stockroom. They expected the shop to be empty tonight.

## Tonight

- Alone in the upstairs counting room at [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/Amekado Sweets\|Amekado Sweets]], waiting for the crew.
- Empty sample jars and a blank recipe book sit on the table. Three failed attempts to imitate the sweets cool on a tray nearby.
- The remaining 120 gp is in a payment envelope in the desk.
- Does not know the party has arrived. Has made no arrangements with city guards.

## If Confronted

- **First response:** Calls it a misunderstanding or a trade inquiry.
- **If shown evidence / the crew:** Tries to reduce it to theft of a recipe; admits hiring them rather than fight armed intruders.
- **If threatened:** Frightened, defensive, willing to pay for damage and negotiate. Not a combatant.
- **If offered a deal:** Can discuss restitution, competition, supplies, or ordinary local business contacts. No automatic supper access or special political connections.
- **Boundary:** Protects his livelihood and reputation; would rather settle privately than explain the tunnel to the watch.
