## At a Glance

Toma captains the *Silver Cicada*, a small cargo airship operating from [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/In'nali\|In'nali's]] Lower Lift Docks. He cares more about getting paid and staying airborne than politics.

## Running Toma

- **Voice:** Dry, clipped, and casually sarcastic.
- **Wants:** Paid work, a sound flight plan, and no unnecessary attention.
- **Useful For:** Discreet passage, modest cargo, pilot-community information, or introductions around the docks.
- **Limit:** He will take a calculated risk, not a suicidal one.

## Connection to Maki

[[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Maki\|Maki Maki]] earned Toma's respect by buying drinks, trading stories, and making himself useful around the docks.

**Favors owed: 3**

Reasonable favors include:

- Discreet passage aboard the *Silver Cicada*
- Moving a person or modest cargo without many questions
- Information or an introduction within In'nali's pilot community
