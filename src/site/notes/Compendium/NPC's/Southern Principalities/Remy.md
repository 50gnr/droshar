## At a Glance

A rat-like yokai chef and the public face of [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/Maki's Candy Shop\|Maki's Candy Shop]] in [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/In'nali\|In'nali]], employed by [[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Maki\|Maki]]. Remy makes the sweets; a salesperson and bookkeeper handle their own work at the business.

- **Appearance:** Grey-brown fur, rounded ears, long whiskers, pink nose, and a long bare tail.
- **Working clothes:** Flour-dusted apron, rolled blue sleeves, patterned headcloth; recipe slips close at hand.
- **Voice:** Practical; talks in quantities, batches, and what needs replacing.
- **Concern:** A functioning kitchen and stock that is where he left it.
- **Office:** The shop's rear loft, also used for storage. Not sleeping quarters.

# Hidden Data

## The Ingredient

- Session 13 established that Maki hired Remy to work the special ingredient into the sweets and made him the product's public face.
- Of the shop's employees, only Remy was to know about the ingredient; he signed a confidentiality agreement.
- Its criminal sources and uncertain long-term effects are not ordinary staff knowledge.
- Do not invent a precise formula, safe dose, ingredient stock quantity, or medical outcome. Those have not been established.

## Session 15 Arrangements

- [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Toma Cloudwake\|Toma Cloudwake]] let him know to expect Maki and companions overnight.
- Has locked up and gone home before they arrive. The staff have an early weekend; nobody is sleeping in the shop.
- The party can use the rear stockroom and its worktable. The loft remains office/storage.
- Returns in the morning to receive [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Shōren Vale\|Shōren Vale]]'s runner and pass the party their papers.
- Knows they are expected; has not been briefed on the assassination.
