## Overview

Renji Maki is the older brother of [[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Maki\|Maki Maki]].

For many years, most believed Ren had simply disappeared.

Whether by choice or circumstance, no reliable record of his whereabouts has surfaced since.

Only recently have rumors begun placing him back within the Southern Principalities.

---

## Description

Like his younger brother, Ren possesses a sharp mind and an observant nature. Age has done little to soften him. He carries himself with the confidence of someone accustomed to making difficult decisions and living with their consequences.

Though they share many physical features, where Maki's presence is warm and inviting, Ren's is measured and quietly unsettling.

He studies people before speaking.

When he finally does...

Every word feels chosen.

---

## Public Reputation

Very little is known.

Among those who claim to have met him recently:

- travels frequently
- asks unusual questions
- pays well for information
- avoids unnecessary attention
- disappears before authorities arrive

Many dismiss the stories as rumor.

Others quietly stop talking when his name is mentioned.

---

## Personality

Ren is intelligent, patient, and remarkably difficult to read.

He prefers understanding a situation before acting and rarely wastes effort on emotional outbursts.

Unlike his brother, Ren has little interest in winning people over.

He would rather understand what motivates them.

---

## Philosophy

> "Truth has a habit of surviving."

Ren believes that every carefully constructed story eventually develops cracks.

His work is simply to find them.

---

## Relationship to [[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Maki\|Maki Maki]]

Ren and Maki have not seen one another for many years.

Whatever once existed between them has long since been replaced by unanswered questions.

Whether reconciliation remains possible...

Neither brother truly knows.

## The Truth

**Earlier canon corrected for Session 16:** In their younger days, Renji paid the Lotus Choir to kill Maki. This was an assassination commission, not merely a false accusation. Shōren Vale chose to recruit Maki instead and ordered Renji disappeared.

### What the Choir Did

- Abducted Renji through intermediaries and confiscated the papers and contacts that would let him resume his old life.
- Kept him in secret confinement for six months, then forced him into supervised bookkeeping at a distant Choir-controlled freight concern for three years.
- Used isolation, threats and controlled movement; no memory alteration, magical geas, or new supernatural restraint is established.
- Renji escaped during a warehouse fire. His handlers presumed him dead; the old case was closed without a recovered body.
- He rebuilt a civilian life and eventually became the reception hall's manager. Exact employment dates and the freight concern's name remain unestablished.

Renji knows the Choir took him. Vale authorized the decision through masked intermediaries; Renji has no basis from this episode to identify Shōren as the Quiet Fox.

His grievance contains real coercion and lost years, but he minimizes his own decision to commission Maki's murder. He believes Maki benefited from his removal and resents the career that followed.

What Maki was told at the time must follow established player knowledge. Do not give him new memories, claim he approved the confinement, or automatically reveal this history when the brothers recognize each other. Whether Vale knows Renji's present employment remains unresolved.

## Goals

Ren is not interested in revenge through violence.

He wants something far more satisfying.

He wants Maki's reputation to collapse under the weight of the truth—or what people believe to be the truth.

If Maki loses the public's trust...

Ren wins.

---

## Story Use

Ren works best through:

- rumors
- anonymous letters
- missing documents
- witnesses from the past
- political embarrassment
- inconvenient truths
- unexpected encounters during downtime

He should rarely attack directly.

His preferred battlefield is public perception.

### Presentation Notes

- Calm.
- Never hurried.
- Speaks softly.
- Holds eye contact.
- Smiles very little.
- Never raises his voice.
- Always seems one step ahead.

### Important

Renji commissioned his brother's murder. His later coercion by the Choir was also real. Neither fact cancels the other.

He presents himself as the wronged brother and withholds his own responsibility. Reveal particulars through actual conversation or evidence, not an automatic explanatory speech.
