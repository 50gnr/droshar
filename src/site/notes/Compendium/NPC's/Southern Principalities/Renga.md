# Renga

> [!note]- Renga and Crew
> ![Renga and Crew.png](/img/user/Assets/Images/NPC/Renga%20and%20Crew.png)
> Left to right: **Renga**, **Hachi**, **Suzu**, **Koma**.

## At a Glance

A human odd-job foreman who takes cellar-clearance, loading, and small demolition work around [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/In'nali\|In'nali]]. Broad shoulders, a patched brown workcoat, and a pencil tucked behind one ear. His usual crew consists of Hachi, Suzu, and Koma.

- **Voice:** Confident working foreman; keeps trying to lower everybody's volume.
- **Habit:** Checks the nearest exit whenever a job starts to look expensive.

# Hidden Data

## Tonight's Job

[[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Genzo Amekado\|Genzo Amekado]] hired the four to steal recipe notes and ingredient samples from [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/Maki's Candy Shop\|Maki's Candy Shop]]. They know who paid them, where the tunnel leads, and what they were asked to take. They do not know what the secret ingredient is.

| Person | Role | At the Table |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Renga | Foreman; enters first | Tries “maintenance” as an explanation; rapidly discovers its limits. |
| Hachi | Digger; follows with tools | Nervous, apologetic, liable to say “Mr. Amekado said it was empty.” |
| Suzu | Collects samples and notes | Practical; would happily abandon the fee to get out safely. |
| Koma | Tunnel lookout | Wants everyone out before anybody starts throwing spells. |

## Running the Crew

- **Want:** Notes, samples, their fee, and an uneventful exit.
- **Expected opposition:** Locked cupboards and perhaps a shopkeeper. Not five level-12 operatives.
- **If discovered:** Excuse, bargain, retreat. Violence is a choice, not their opening requirement.
- **If attacked:** After the first unmistakable display of the party's strength, try to withdraw, help a fallen crewmate, or surrender. No fight to the death.
- **If questioned:** Employer, payment, and route are available without an elaborate interrogation. Nobody was paid enough to protect Genzo with their life.
- **Gear:** Pry bars, short digging tools, sacks, padded sample jars, and a hooded lantern. Their equipment suits burglary rather than assassination.
