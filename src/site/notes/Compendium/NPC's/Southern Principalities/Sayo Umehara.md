A human event planner at the [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/High Circles Reception Hall\|High Circles Reception Hall]]. A plum-coloured sash, ink-stained fingertips, and a remarkable memory for prospective clients' names distinguish her from the bustle around her.

- **Appearance:** Around forty; warm brown eyes, black hair in a practical braided bun, charcoal-grey patterned robes and a wide plum sash. Carries a bound appointment book.
- **Voice:** Warm, assured, attentive; keeps selling the venue while resolving interruptions.
- **Wants:** Satisfied clients, a booking, and preparations that finish on time.
- **Useful for:** Tours, private-event arrangements, catering and security introductions.
