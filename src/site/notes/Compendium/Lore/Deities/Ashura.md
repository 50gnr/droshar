# Ashura

## The Fallen God

Ashura was a three-faced, six-armed demon god of destruction. Legend says the other deities cast him down from the heavens after he offended them.

Ashura himself is believed to be gone. His power and legacy survive through the fragments left by his defeat.

## Fragment of Fury

When Ashura fell, his being was broken apart and scattered across the world. Ancient seals contained those fragments, but time has steadily weakened them.

Physical **Aspects of Ashura** can manifest where bloodlust has left a deep enough mark:

- Caverns near recently watered battlefields
- Sewers beneath arenas whose crowds hunger for violence
- Crypts beneath temples of ritual sacrifice

These manifestations are echoes of Ashura rather than the original god returned whole.

## Aspects of Ashura

An Aspect carries a distorted portion of Ashura's demonic magic and battle prowess. Its emotions exist at their most extreme: happiness becomes ecstasy, devotion becomes zealotry, and anger collapses into blinding rage.

An unrestrained Aspect offers no mercy or surrender once battle begins. Death is normally the only reliable way to stop one.

## Known Connection

The hidden [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Ashura Compound\|Ashura Compound]] shelters the [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Aspect of Ashura\|Waiting Aspect]], which has voluntarily sealed itself in contemplation. Why it chose restraint, what it awaits, and how long that restraint can last remain unknown.

[[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Clay\|Clay]] carries a shard associated with Ashura embedded within him. Whether it is one of the original Fragments of Fury, a piece of an Aspect, or something created from the same power has not been established.

> [!note]- DM Boundary
> Do not treat Clay as an Aspect of Ashura by default. The shard establishes a connection, not the answer to his origin.
