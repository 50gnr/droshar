# The Golden Company

## At a Glance

An organized adventuring company that entered the flooded [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Hakuryū Valley\|Hakuryū Valley]] with boats, healers, historians, and supplies for stranded settlements.

## What They Did

- Survived their first confrontation with [[Compendium/Lore/Deities/Kaiju/Bakuryō\|Bakuryō]]
- Withdrew to study the old stories rather than immediately attack again
- Returned with a plan to reach the reversed scale beneath Bakuryō's jaw
- Endured long enough to turn the scale and restore the river dragon

## Clay's Account — The Scale Righted

The Golden Company drew Bakuryō away from the surviving settlements and fought her through most of the day. They never came close to overpowering the river dragon. Their boats kept moving through the reversed current, their healers pulled the wounded back into the fight, and the rest of the company endured long enough for one of them to reach the chevron-shaped scale beneath her jaw.

The moment the scale was forced back into place, Bakuryō stopped. The fury left her eyes and she lowered her head before those still standing. Around them, the Hakuryū hesitated—then began flowing downhill again.

## Connection to Clay

[[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Clay\|Clay]] fought beside the company, helped limit their losses during the failed first attempt, and contributed to their final victory.
