# High Circles Reception Hall

A prestigious reception venue in [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/In'nali\|In'nali]]'s Upper City, available for private ceremonies between civic functions. Broad stone steps lead into a carved cedar entrance beneath deep tiled eaves. A long reception salon runs across the windowed frontage, opening through broad arches into the deeper banquet hall. Substantial side wings hold the private and working rooms; an upper gallery overlooks the public spaces.

**Wedding consultations:** [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Sayo Umehara\|Sayo Umehara]].  
**Magical services:** [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Iori Sazanami\|Iori Sazanami]].

Estimated from the cropped perspective exterior and person-sized furniture/doorways, not a measured survey. This broader revised layout supersedes the original 140 × 100-foot plan and the initial 180 × 140-foot redesign estimate.
- **Front reception salon:** Approximately 220 × 30 feet across the entire glazed frontage. Sofas and small tables around the perimeter; broad circulation between the central street entrance, stairs, side-wing passages, and three banquet-hall arches.
- **Central hall:** Six widely spaced round tables and a principal table at the north end. Pale stone floor, structural columns, and ample circulation. Fifty invited guests remains the event count; the furniture shown is not a revised guest census.
- **West wing:** Tasting/lounge room behind the salon; kitchen farther north; pantry and loading court at the northwest rear. Inner service corridor links the rooms and west hall doors.
- **East wing:** Private reception parlour behind the salon, with its own east street entrance. Manager's office immediately north of it, entered from the inner east corridor, not through the parlour. Cloakroom/washroom and stores farther north.
- **Rear:** Cross-passage links loading, kitchen, stores, side corridors, and service stairs without crossing the banquet floor.
- **Upper:** Gallery along the hall's front and both sides, also overlooking the front salon. West dressing suites and linen/furniture stores; east consultation room, Iori's workroom, and stores.
- **Stairs:** Public L-shaped stair at the southwest end of the salon: enter beside the south windows, rise WEST along the south wall to the southwest corner landing, then turn NORTH and rise along the west wall onto the upper walkway. From upstairs, descend south, then east along the windows. Service stair at the northeast rear is unchanged. Both connections match between the two maps.

## Security Mage — GM Reference

**Ordinary appointment:** Iori is working, not continuously screening all wedding clients. No blanket detection field or permanent anti-magic effect covers the hall.

**Signed supper contract — active baseline; the proposed upgrade below is not commissioned:**

- **17:30:** Iori casts [[3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/see-invisibility-xphb\|See Invisibility]]; normal one-hour duration, through 18:30. No automatic continuous renewal is scheduled.
- **18:00–18:30:** At the main-entrance check-in in the front salon, maintains [[3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/detect-magic-xphb\|Detect Magic]] through three consecutive ten-minute castings, concentration permitting. Its thirty-foot range does not cover the whole salon or every entrance. Physical guest/staff checks continue alongside it.
- **After main arrivals:** Moves between the hall, gallery, and workroom as required; detection is not permanently running. Can investigate a specific concern using remaining resources.
- **Reserve:** Two 3rd-level castings shared between [[3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/dispel-magic-xphb\|Dispel Magic]] and [[3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/counterspell-xphb\|Counterspell]]. Count uses; no unlimited reactions or dispels.
- [[3-Mechanics/CLI/spells/alarm-xphb\|Alarm]] protects her equipment cupboard after hours, not the manager's office or every doorway.

**What the checks actually mean:**

- Enchanted jewellery and other declared, permitted items are expected among wealthy guests. Detecting magic does not itself prove hostile intent.
- Weapons are handled separately by Etsu's guards. Service access is checked against a roster; neither a delivery nor a vendor's approval grants unrestricted access for companions.
- Detect Magic senses magical effects within thirty feet; viewing an aura requires a visible creature/object and reveals a spell's school, not its name, purpose, or creator. Use the linked 2024 spell's barriers: one foot of stone/dirt/wood, one inch of metal, or a thin sheet of lead.
- A concealed transformed creature is not automatically identified by species or located inside an opaque object. Its magic can still be sensed if in range and not blocked under the spell.
- See Invisibility reveals invisible/ethereal creatures and objects. It does not grant truesight or automatically reveal a polymorphed creature's original form.
- No commissioned truesight, antimagic field, mind-reading, universal shapechanger detector, or automatic poison ward. Do not invent a new protection in response to the party's chosen plan.
- An unexplained magical effect can prompt questions or a request for examination. Dispel Magic is not casually applied to every guest's belongings.

### Circle-Supported Watch

- Iori would act as guardian/coordinator; two junior ritual assistants would maintain a fixed screening station.
- Initial homebrew proposal: extend a normal Detect Magic-style watch to a maximum four-hour shift, anchored at one station. Thirty-foot coverage from that station, normal barriers and visibility limits.
- This proposes duration support, not stronger DCs, automatic identification of spells, true forms, or building-wide detection. Amplification beyond duration remains unresolved.
- Assistants must remain at the station and concentrate; interruption ends the sustained watch. They do not simultaneously maintain unrelated spells.
- This is a proposed special ritual, not a claim that ordinary spellcasting can share concentration or extend spells this way.
- The two assistants are not in the current twenty-person venue staff count or today's tour roster.

### Witnessglass — Working Item Concept

- A proposed observation item, not a finalized magic-item stat block or ordinary Scrying spell.
- Up to three fixed, installed viewing seals; Iori selects **one live view at a time**. Each view is a fixed ordinary visual perspective from its seal.
- Initial limits: no sound, recording, playback, seeing through walls, free roaming, or automatic recognition of disguised/transformed creatures.
- Ordinary obstructions block the view. See Invisibility cast on the viewer does not automatically apply to the remote feed.
- No spells cast through it. Remote sight does not satisfy Counterspell's requirement to see a caster within range of Iori herself.
- Installation points, vulnerability and final mechanics must be set openly in the GM reference before it becomes an active protection; do not add a hidden seal in response to their chosen route.
- A protected viewing station upstairs is possible if commissioned. It replaces some of Iori's physical attendance; it does not put her in two places at once.
