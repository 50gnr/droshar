# Rootglass Conservatory

> [!quote|no-t] SUMMARY
> A garden that never closes, where Bardune's Hanimori cultivate rare life and recover their own.

**Type:** Public Conservatory, Research Garden, Nursery, and Clinic  
**Location:** [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/Bardune/Bardune\|Bardune]]  
**Primary Community:** Hanimori  
**Status:** Open and active

## At a Glance

Rootglass is one of Bardune's oldest Hanimori conservatories. Curved roofs of glass, cedar, and pale stone surround an ancient living tree whose roots pass through the terraces and disappear into the foundations below.

The conservatory is not merely a greenhouse. It is a botanical research facility, a nursery for rare and spirit-sensitive plants, a clinic, and a place of rejuvenation for Hanimori whose health depends upon carefully maintained cycles of light, soil, water, and rest.

Visitors are welcome throughout most of the public gardens, provided they move quietly and respect the patients receiving care.

## First Impression

Warm, damp air settles over the skin as soon as the doors open. Water moves through shallow stone channels between beds of medicinal flowers, moss-covered roots, and carefully labelled fungi. Cedar walks curve around the trunk of the great tree rather than cutting across its growth.

Hanimori attendants work without hurry. Some prune or water the gardens; others help patients settle into beds of living soil beneath filtered sunlight. Conversations remain low, and even busy work carries the stillness of a shrine.

## Public Areas

### The Great Root Hall

The central glass chamber was constructed around the conservatory's ancient tree. Its exposed roots form natural benches, partitions, and raised beds without having been cut from the living trunk.

### Rejuvenation Terraces

Warm terraces of living soil, clean water, and carefully controlled light serve Hanimori patients recovering from injury, sickness, exhaustion, or transplantation.

### Medicinal Galleries

Researchers cultivate rare plants, medicinal fungi, and roots sensitive to nearby spirits. Portions of the collection are available to physicians, shrine keepers, and approved healers throughout Bardune.

### Water Courts

Shallow pools and narrow channels regulate the conservatory's humidity. Small bridges connect the gardens while allowing water and root systems to pass uninterrupted beneath them.

## What the Party Has Learned

- Rootglass houses all four of the Stillwater Array's living Anchors.
- Civic plans show a staircase descending into a lower foundation that is absent from the public record.
- A Hanimori groundskeeper-engineer is responsible for both the conservatory and whatever is maintained below it.
- On the appointed night, four matching nursery carts will leave Rootglass separately and follow different routes out of Bardune.
- Entering the conservatory carelessly would endanger patients and violate a place Bardune treats as both hospital and sanctuary.
