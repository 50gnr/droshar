# Maki's Candy Shop

**Owner:** [[Compendium/Party/Player Characters/Player Facing/Maki\|Maki Maki]]  
**Chef / Public Face:** [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Remy\|Remy]]  
**Location:** Commercial streets on [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/In'nali\|In'nali]]'s plateau, above the cliff; not the settlements beneath the Great Lift.

Maki bought the business for **1,500 gp** during the downtime recorded in [[Session Notes/C2 Southern Principalities/Recap/Session 13 - Recap\|Session 13 - Recap]], then invested further in its presentation and staff. The running sign/name joke is **M&M&M&M&M&M…**, with a variable number of M's; no fixed full trading name has been established. This page retains its descriptive title.

## Layout

- **Front shop:** Narrow frontage, tall display windows, and a deep interior. A two-story vaulted ceiling rises above colourful jars, high shelving, hanging displays, and a long sales counter.
- **Preparation kitchen:** Behind the sales area; work surfaces, cooling racks, cookware, and ordinary confectionery supplies.
- **Rear stockroom:** Sacks, crates, ingredient shelves, and a substantial worktable. The party's overnight planning and bedroll space.
- **Rear loft:** Remy's office, business records, and spare stock; reached by an internal stair. Looks toward the open front of the shop. No sleeping space.
- **Doors:** Front customer entrance and a rear delivery entrance. Maki has ordinary owner access.

## Staff

- [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Remy\|Remy]] — chef and public face.
- A salesperson and a bookkeeper — established hires; names not yet established.

# Hidden Data

## Special Sweets

- Maki arranged for a refined substance derived from glowing stone mined below In'nali to be included in the sweets. He found two criminal supply contacts during Session 13.
- Only Remy among the employees was to know the ingredient. Long-term effects remain unresolved.
- Before the burglary, let Maki establish any precautions or storage arrangements for sensitive stock. Do not assume the thieves obtain it.

## Tonight — Session 15

- Remy has closed up and gone home after being told to expect the party by [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Toma Cloudwake\|Toma Cloudwake]]. Staff have an early weekend.
- Party accommodation: rear stockroom, not the loft. Ownership provides a place to gather; it does not make this an established covert safe house.
- [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Shōren Vale\|Shōren Vale]]'s runner brings ordinary city/travel papers to Remy the following morning. Venue admission remains separate.

## Burglary Route

- [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Genzo Amekado\|Genzo Amekado]], owner of nearby [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/Amekado Sweets\|Amekado Sweets]], commissioned [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Renga\|Renga]]'s crew to steal recipes and ingredient samples.
- They reopened a disused passage through the nearby cellars and are making the final breach beneath the stockroom floor.
- Narrow, single-file route; roughly sixty feet including the final crawl. Runs beneath this short row of premises, not out of the city.
- It ends in Amekado Sweets' cellar. Its steps lead up to the rival's workroom and shop.
- The break-in and discovery of the route are prepared possibilities, not events that have happened.
