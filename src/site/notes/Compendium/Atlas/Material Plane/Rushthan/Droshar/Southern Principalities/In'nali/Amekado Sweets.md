# Amekado Sweets

**Proprietor:** [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Genzo Amekado\|Genzo Amekado]]  
**Location:** A few premises from [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/Maki's Candy Shop\|Maki's Candy Shop]], among [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Rushthan/Droshar/Southern Principalities/In'nali/In'nali\|In'nali]]'s plateau businesses.

An established confectionery with a green awning, orderly trays of sesame brittle, and carefully stacked boxes of preserved-fruit sweets. The hanging wooden sign bears the shop's name and a painted twist of pulled sugar.

## Layout

- **Front:** Modest sales room; display counter, wrapped orders, and a locked street door after closing.
- **Rear:** Workroom with cooling trays, ingredient cupboards, a delivery door, and stairs down to the cellar.
- **Cellar:** Storage shelves, tools, and an old passage entrance.
- **Upstairs:** A small counting room overlooking the street, reached from the rear workroom.

## Tonight

- Genzo is alone upstairs awaiting [[Compendium/NPC's/Southern Principalities/Renga\|Renga]]'s crew. Staff have gone home.
- No guards, hired reinforcements, or magical security have been established for this ordinary business.
- The reopened cellar passage reaches the breach beneath Maki's stockroom. Following it does not require a dungeon exploration sequence.

## Arrival from the Tunnel

> The cramped passage opens between empty packing crates. A hooded lantern stands beside a heap of fresh rubble; above it, wooden steps climb toward a strip of warm light. The air smells of sesame and cooling caramel.
>
> Somewhere overhead, a chair scrapes. “Well? Did you get it?”

Use the voice only if the party has made enough noise for Genzo to mistake them for the returning crew. If they approach silently, he remains upstairs.

## Evidence Available

- Fresh excavation and hired tools in his cellar.
- Empty sample jars, a blank recipe book, and unsuccessful imitation batches in the counting room.
- An envelope holding the promised **120 gp** balance in the desk.
- The crew can identify their employer. A special check is not required to establish a connection already made obvious by following his tunnel.
