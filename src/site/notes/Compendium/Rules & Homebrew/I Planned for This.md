# Replacing Inspiration: “I Planned for This”

Yes. This campaign is a perfect place to replace ordinary Inspiration with something that reinforces the party being experienced Lotus Choir operatives.

## Contingency Token

Each player may hold **one Contingency Token**.

Award one for:

- exceptional character play;
- a clever operational decision;
- creating a memorable table moment;
- turning failure into something entertaining;
- helping another character succeed in a way that changes the scene.

Nobu absolutely earned one this session.

## Spend: “I Planned for This”

A player spends the token and briefly explains what their character prepared earlier.

They may introduce one **reasonable, minor contingency**, such as:

- a useful mundane item;
- a rope, disguise component, tool, or concealed pouch;
- having positioned something nearby;
- remembering a useful operational detail;
- having previously arranged a minor signal or escape measure;
- gaining advantage on one check because of prior preparation.

It cannot:

- create a magic item;
- produce an unprepared spell;
- establish a powerful NPC ally;
- undo an event that has already happened;
- bypass the central challenge of a scene.

The declaration has to feel like something a veteran operative could genuinely have anticipated.

## Examples

> “I planned for this. Before leaving the wagon, I tied a second climbing line beneath the bridge.”

> “I planned for this. I packed waxed sleeves for the documents.”

> “I planned for this. I left one of our identifying banners rolled up instead of mounted.”

> “I planned for this. During reconnaissance, I marked the safest route back with chalk beneath the railing.”
