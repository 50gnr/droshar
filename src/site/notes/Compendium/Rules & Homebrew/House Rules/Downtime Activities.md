# Downtime Activities

While your character is not adventuring, they can perform downtime activities. Downtime is measured in **workweeks of 5 days**.

Days spent on a downtime activity don't need to be consecutive unless the activity states otherwise. Some activities can be completed by multiple characters working together.

> [!column]
>
>> [!NOTE]
>> 
>> ## Buy a Magic Item
>>
>> Spend time and money searching for a magic item that is available for sale.
>>
>> Make a **Charisma (Persuasion) check** to determine whether you locate an item. +1 bonus to the check for each week beyond the first and each +100gp up to +10.
>>
>> **Resources. At least 1 workweek and 100 gp**.
>>
>> | Item Rarity | Scroll Level | DC |
>> | --- | --- | ---: |
>> | Common | 1st | 10 |
>> | Uncommon | 2nd–3rd | 15 |
>> | Rare | 4th–5th | 20 |
>> | Very Rare | 6th–8th | 25 |
>> | Legendary | 9th | 30 |
>>
>> ---
>>
>> ## Carousing
>>
>> Spend time drinking, eating and socialising. Perhaps you can make some new friends, or enemies. 
>> 
>> Requires a Persuasion ability check.
>> 
>> **Resources: 1 workweek and 10 gp to go carousing with the lower classes, or 50 gp to carouse with the middle classes. Carousing with the upper classes requires 250 gp—and access to the local nobility.**
>>
>> ---
>>
>> ## Crafting & Reforging
>>
>>Use an appropriate tool proficiency to create or modify an item. 
>>
>>**Resources. To determine the number of days required to craft an item, divide its cost in gp by 50**. 
>>You must have the appropriate tools, a suitable work area, and raw materials equal in value to half of the item's cost.
>>
>> ### Potions
>> 
>> A character proficient with a **Herbalism Kit** can craft the following potions. 
>> Crafting costs are equal to **half the potion's Sane market price**. 
>> 
>> | Potion | Rarity | Time | Crafting Cost | 
>> | --- | --- | --- | ---: | 
>> | Potion of Healing | Common | 1 day | 25 gp | 
>> | Potion of Greater Healing | Uncommon | 1 workweek | 75 gp | 
>> | Potion of Superior Healing | Very Rare | 3 workweeks | 225 gp | 
>> | Potion of Supreme Healing | Very Rare | 4 workweeks | 675 gp | 
>> 
>> ### Magic Items 
>> 
>> Crafting or reforging a magic item requires appropriate components in addition to the necessary time, materials, and tool proficiency. The components required depend on the properties of the item being created or modified. 
>> 
>> ## Crime
>> 
>> Sometimes crime pays, but you always run the risk of being arrested. Requires ability checks for Stealth and Thieves' Tools, and also your choice of either the Deception, Investigation, or Perception skills.
>> **Resources: Planning a crime takes 1 workweek and 25 gp, which is spent planning and gathering information about potential targets.**
>> 
>> ## Gambling 
>> 
>>An easy way to make a fortune, or lose one. Requires Deception, Insight, and Intimidation ability checks. If you are proficient with a relevant gaming set, that skill can replace one of the other checks.
>>**Resources: 1 workweek and a stake of at least 10 gp.**
>> 
>>
>
>> [!NOTE]
>> 
>> ## Pit Fighting 
>> 
>> Compete in boxing, wrestling, and other forms of (usually) non-lethal combat, in an organized setting. Requires Acrobatics, Athletics, and Constitution ability checks. You can replace any one of those ability checks with an attack roll made using one of your weapons.
>> **Resources: 1 workweek.**
>> 
>> ## Relaxation
>>
>>Take a break to relax and recover from sickness or a critical injury.
>>**Resources: 1 workweek, during which time you need to maintain at least a modest lifestyle in order to gain the benefits of this activity.**
>>
>> ---
>>
>> ## Religious Service
>>
>>Spend time in service of, and try to win favor with, a religion or the like. Requires either a Religion or Persuasion ability check.
>>**Resources: 1 workweek, and some kind of temple, religious group, deity or entity whose beliefs and ethos are aligned with your own.**
>>
>> ---
>>
>> ## Research
>>
>>Try and uncover lore concerning a specific place, person, item etc. Requires an Intelligence ability check. Access to a good library or a sage gives you advantage, and spending more gold gives you a bonus.
>>**Resources: 1 workweek and at least 50 gp. (+1 per 100 gp spent)**
>>
>> ## Sell a Magic Item 
>> 
>> Spread word that you have a magic item for sale, and try to find the best offer currently available. Requires a Persuasion ability check.
>> **Resources: 1 workweek and 25 gp.**
>> The item's base value is determined using the **Sane Magic Item Prices** list. 
>> 
>> 
>> | **Persuasion Check Total** | **1-5** | **6-10** | **11-15** | **16-20** | **21+** |
>> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | 
>> | **% of Item Value Offered** | 50% | 75% | 100% | 125% | 150% |
>> 
>> ## Scribe a Spell Scroll 
>> 
>> Use your Arcana proficiency to enscribe a spell you know on a scroll.
>> **Resources: The time and cost is based on the level of the spell. You must also provide all of the material components required for casting the spell—all of which are consumed in scribing the scroll.**
>>
>>| Spell Level | Time | Cost | 
>>| ---: | --- | ---: | 
>>| 0 | 1 hour | 5 gp |
>>| 1st | 1 day | 30 gp | 
>>| 2nd | 3 days | 60 gp | 
>>| 3rd | 1 workweek | 100 gp | 
>>| 4th | 2 workweeks |  160 gp | 
>>| 5th | 4 workweeks | 320 gp | 
>>| 6th | 8 workweeks | 640 gp | 
>>| 7th | 16 workweeks | 1280 gp | 
>>| 8th | 32 workweeks | 2560 gp | 
>>| 9th | 48 workweeks | 5120 gp |
>>
>>## Training 
>>
>>Spend time learning a new **language or tool proficiency**. 
>>**Resources. - 10 workweeks, minus your Intelligence modifier, and 25 gp per workweek. Training is only possible from a proficient tutor.** 
>>
>>## Work 
>>
>>Find temporary work to cover your general living costs. Requires an Athletics, Acrobatics, or Intelligence ability check if you use a set of tools, or a Performance check if you are providing entertainment.
>>**Resources: 1 workweek.**
>>


# Hidden Data

Use these rules to resolve downtime activities after a player declares how their character spends their available downtime.

---

## Buy a Magic Item P.126

The character makes a **Charisma (Persuasion) check**.

| Check | Items Found |
| ---: | --- |
| 1–5 | Roll 1d6 times on Magic Item Table A |
| 6–10 | Roll 1d4 times on Magic Item Table B |
| 11–15 | Roll 1d4 times on Magic Item Table C |
| 16–20 | Roll 1d4 times on Magic Item Table D |
| 21–25 | Roll 1d4 times on Magic Item Table E |
| 26–30 | Roll 1d4 times on Magic Item Table F |
| 31–35 | Roll 1d4 times on Magic Item Table G |
| 36–40 | Roll 1d4 times on Magic Item Table H |
| 41+ | Roll 1d4 times on Magic Item Table I |

If the character is searching for a **specific magic item**, include that item among those available if the check is high enough to locate an item of its rarity.

**Complication.** 10% chance per workweek spent searching.

---

## Carousing  P.128

The character chooses the social class with which they carouse and makes a **Charisma (Persuasion) check**.

Contacts should be appropriate to the social class selected.

A contact remains useful until circumstances change or the character abuses the relationship.

**Complication.** 10% chance per workweek.

---

## Crafting & Reforging P.128

### Mundane Items

A character produces **50 gp of progress per day** of work.

The character must provide raw materials worth **half the finished item's value**.

Multiple characters can contribute to the same item if each has the required proficiency.

### Potions

Use the potion crafting time and costs listed in the player section.

### Magic Items

Resolve magic-item crafting and reforging using **Heliana's Guide**.

Appropriate monster components are required according to the properties being added to the item.

---

## Crime P.130

The character makes three checks:

1. **Dexterity (Stealth)**
2. A check using **Thieves' Tools**
3. **Charisma (Deception), Intelligence (Investigation), or Wisdom (Perception)**

The player chooses the target DC:

| DC | Target Value |
| ---: | ---: |
| 10 | 50 gp |
| 15 | 100 gp |
| 20 | 200 gp |
| 25 | 1,000 gp |

Resolve the crime based on the number of successful checks.

| Successes | Result |
| ---: | --- |
| 0 | Character is caught and jailed |
| 1 | Crime fails; character escapes |
| 2 | Crime succeeds for half the target value |
| 3 | Crime succeeds for the full target value |

**Jail.** The character spends **1 week in jail for each 25 gp of the target value** and can be released by paying a fine equal to the target value.

**Complication.**  If they only succeed on one check or more roll **1D8** on the Crime Complications Table.

---

## Gambling P.130

The character chooses a stake between **10 gp and 1,000 gp**.

Make three checks:

- Wisdom (Insight)
- Charisma (Deception)
- Charisma (Intimidation)

The DC for each check is **5 + 2d10**, rolled separately.

| Successes | Result |
| ---: | --- |
| 0 | Lose the entire stake and accrue a debt equal to the stake |
| 1 | Lose half the stake |
| 2 | Gain half the stake |
| 3 | Gain an amount equal to the stake |

**Complication.** 10% chance per workweek. 

---

## Pit Fighting

Make three checks:

- **Strength (Athletics)**
- **Dexterity (Acrobatics)**
- **Constitution check**

One check can be replaced by an **attack roll using a weapon**.

The DC for each check is **5 + 2d10**, rolled separately.

| Successes | Earnings |
| ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 0 gp |
| 1 | 50 gp |
| 2 | 100 gp |
| 3 | 200 gp |

**Complication.** 10% chance per workweek.

---

## Relaxation

After one workweek of relaxation, the character gains the following benefits:

- Advantage on saving throws against diseases and poisons for the following week.
- At the end of the week, the character can end one effect preventing them from regaining hit points.
- At the end of the week, the character can restore one ability score that has been reduced below its normal value.

The latter two benefits don't apply to effects with an ongoing duration of 30 days or longer.

---

## Religious Service

The character makes either an **Intelligence (Religion)** or **Charisma (Persuasion)** check.

| Check | Favors Earned |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1–10 | 0 |
| 11–20 | 1 |
| 21+ | 2 |

A character can have a maximum of **1 + their Charisma modifier** unused favors, minimum 1.

A favor can represent assistance reasonably available from the organization, such as:

- Information
- Temporary assistance
- Minor services
- Political or social support

**Complication.** 10% chance per workweek.

---

## Research

The character makes an **Intelligence check**.

Apply a **+1 bonus for each additional 100 gp spent**, to a maximum of +6.

Access to an appropriate library or knowledgeable sage can grant advantage.

| Check | Lore Learned |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1–5 | No effect |
| 6–10 | 1 piece |
| 11–20 | 2 pieces |
| 21+ | 3 pieces |

Each piece of lore represents **one useful fact** concerning the subject being researched.

For each additional workweek spent researching, the character can attempt another check.

**Complication.** 10% chance per workweek.

---

## Sell a Magic Item P.134

The character makes a **Charisma (Persuasion) check**.

Apply the result to the item's **Sane Magic Item Price**.

| Check | Offer |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1–5 | 50% |
| 6–10 | 75% |
| 11–15 | 100% |
| 16–20 | 125% |
| 21+ | 150% |

**Complication.** 10% chance per workweek.

---

## Scribe a Spell Scroll

No check is normally required.

If the character has the required spell, Arcana proficiency, time, gold, and material components, the scroll is successfully created.

Use the time and cost table in the player section.

**Complication.** None unless circumstances warrant one.

---

## Training

No check is normally required.

After completing the required training time and paying the associated costs, the character gains the selected **language or tool proficiency**.

Training requires an appropriate instructor.

**Complication.** 10% chance per 10 workweeks.

---

## Work

The character makes one appropriate ability check:

- Strength (Athletics)
- Dexterity (Acrobatics)
- Intelligence using an appropriate tool proficiency
- Charisma (Performance)

| Check | Earnings |
| ---: | --- |
| 9 or lower | 1.5 gp |
| 10–14 | 7 gp |
| 15–20 | 14 gp |
| 21+ | 39 gp |

**Complication.** 10% chance per workweek.
