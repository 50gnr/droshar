 # Warlock: Elder Sphinx Patron

_Consort with the Enigmatic Mind of the Ancients_

Warlocks bound to elder sphinxes—timeless, inscrutable creatures with control over the tides of possibility—often become as cryptic and enigmatic as their masters.

For those who enter a pact for a noble cause, such as joining a sphinx like Aeon in its eternal vigil over time, this transformation is a mark of pride. The less fortunate few who are pressed into service after losing a contest of riddles, on the other hand, resentfully resist this creeping change.

In the end, exposure to the unfathomable mysteries inherent to the very nature of a sphinx inexorably alters those who serve it—and yet many still flock to do so, lured by the promise of forbidden knowledge and ancient secrets. No matter where, when, or who you are, if you are burdened with burning questions, an elder sphinx almost certainly holds the answers. Obtaining them, however, is a different matter altogether.

For better or worse, elder sphinxes are aloof and inscrutable patrons, often more preoccupied with studying the currents of time than the insignificant concerns of their mortal Warlocks. It is hard to stir them to action, and harder still to convince them to part with the vast knowledge they hold—which they do only in the form of riddles, and never without purpose.

Yet for the patient, perceptive, and persistently curious, there is much to be gleaned from the teachings of such ancient beings. The path of the elder sphinx offers little in the way of gold or glory… but it may just unlock the deepest secrets of the multiverse.

## Twist of Fate

_Level 3 Elder Sphinx Feature_

Your mind becomes attuned to the swirling whims of fate, allowing you to nudge destiny in unusual directions that others can’t foresee while simultaneously standing firm in the chaos.

After you or an ally you can see within 30 feet makes a D20 Test but before the GM announces the outcome, you can alter the roll by shifting the d20 to an adjacent face (no action required), to which you then add applicable modifiers as normal.

If you don’t have a physical d20, refer to the table below.

|10|8, 12, 17|20|2, 8, 14|

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Enigmatic Soul

_Level 3 Elder Sphinx Feature_

You study under a master of riddles, becoming as inscrutable and timeless as your tutor.

You can’t be magically aged, and you are immune to magic that allows other creatures to:

- read your thoughts,
    
- determine whether you are lying,
    
- know your alignment, or
    
- know your creature type.

## Branching Possibilities

_Level 6 Elder Sphinx Feature_

Channelling your patron’s magic, you can split the timeline into multiple potential futures and choose the path that hinders your opponent the most.

When you are attacked while concentrating on a spell, you can take a Reaction to roll a d6.

- **1–2:** The attack continues as normal.
    
- **3–4:** The attacker has Disadvantage on the attack roll.
    
- **5–6:** The attacker must choose a new target or lose the attack.
    

You can use your **Twist of Fate** feature on this d6 roll, using the table below if you don’t have a physical d6.

### D6 Sides

|d6|Adjacent Faces|
|:-:|:--|
|1 or 6|2, 3, 4, 5|
|2 or 5|1, 3, 4, 6|
|3 or 4|1, 2, 5, 6|

## Optional Feature: Three Riddles, Three Answers

If you are using the **Paradox System**, the GM can add this optional level 6 feature to the Elder Sphinx subclass.

You and at least two other willing creatures can link hands and perform a 1-hour ritual to study the integrity of the timeline and anticipate the results of meddling with it.

Draw three cards from the Paradox Deck. Of those cards:

1. Choose one card to remove from the deck and replace with a new card from your current Paradox Tier, which is shuffled into the deck.
    
2. Choose one card to put on the bottom of the deck.
    
3. Choose one card to leave on top of the deck.
    

You then gain **1 Paradox Point**.

Once you perform this ritual, you can’t do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

## Multiple Choice

_Level 10 Elder Sphinx Feature_

Your power over time gives you dominion over every possible outcome during a critical moment.

When you make a D20 Test or a damage roll, you can choose to reroll the dice two more times and choose one of the three results.

If multiple dice are rolled as part of a damage roll, you reroll all of them simultaneously, taking their sum as one of the results.

Once you’ve used this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

---

## Timewalker

_Level 10 Elder Sphinx Feature_

You are a traveller through time, adrift in its mysterious currents.

You always have the _Timewalk*_ spell prepared. You can cast it once without expending a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in this way when you finish a Long Rest.

Alternatively, when you gain your **Mystic Arcanum** class feature, you can expend a use of one of your arcanum spells to cast the _Timewalk_ spell without expending a spell slot, but as if you expended a spell slot of the same level as the chosen arcanum spell.

## Chronal Stasis

_Level 14 Elder Sphinx Feature_

Your mastery of chronomancy allows you to freeze yourself and a foe in time for a brief moment.

When a creature you can see within 60 feet of you starts its turn, you can take a Reaction to force it to make a Wisdom saving throw against your Warlock spell save DC.

On a failure, the creature is **Stunned** until the end of your next turn.

You are **Stunned** while the creature is Stunned in this way, but you don’t lose concentration on spells or other effects as a result.

After a creature fails its saving throw against this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
