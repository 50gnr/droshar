# Cleric: Judgement Domain

_Bear Divine Justice and Punishment_

The foremost Clerics of the gods of judgement, such as Ammit, Anubis, Cerberus, and Rashnu, serve as preachers, judges, and executioners.

They weigh the sins of supplicants, read their ultimate fates in crystal balls and religious trances to describe the paradises or agonies that await... and helpfully offer to deliver absolution through punishment.

Even the kindest of these Clerics have a reputation for unflinching candour and righteous severity—no crime is too petty to condemn, no transgression too small to acknowledge.

The mission statement of a Judgement Cleric is quite simple: **to reward the just and rebuke the wicked.** Significantly less simple is agreeing on who exactly qualifies as either.

Clerics of rival deities hold predictably conflicting views on the nature of morality and righteousness, but even followers of the same god can interpret the same doctrine in wildly different ways. Their disputes—often heated and contentious—draw the attention of moral philosophers the world over, who flock to the grandest of these clashes in droves to cheer for their favourites and place wagers on the outcome.

## Pass Judgement

_Level 3 Judgement Domain Feature_

As a Bonus Action, you can commend or rebuke the actions of a creature you can see within 30 feet of you.

The next time that creature hits with an attack roll within 1 minute, roll **2d4** and add the result to the damage roll if you commended it, or subtract the result from the damage roll if you rebuked it.

This roll increases when you reach certain Cleric levels:

|Cleric Level|Judgement Dice|
|--:|:-:|
|3|2d4|
|5|2d6|
|9|2d8|
|13|2d10|
|17|2d12|

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## Eyes of Truth

_Level 3 Judgement Domain Feature_

You see truths about others that they pray remain hidden—and your insight into their very souls tells you what they’re about to do.

As a Bonus Action, you press your Holy Symbol to your forehead and expend a use of your Channel Divinity to open your senses to the true nature of those around you.

For 1 minute, you can sense the alignment of all creatures within 60 feet of you that aren’t behind Total Cover.

When you use this feature, choose one alignment to condemn:

- **Evil**
    
- **Good**
    
- **Neutral**
    

For the duration, each creature you can sense using this feature that is of the chosen alignment has Disadvantage on the first attack roll it makes against you each turn.

In addition, you can use your **Pass Judgement** feature without expending a use of it to rebuke creatures you can sense using this feature that are of the chosen alignment.

## Wages of Sin

_Level 6 Judgement Domain Feature_

When an enemy you can see within 30 feet of you forces one or more creatures to make a saving throw, you can take a Reaction to force that enemy to make the same saving throw, suffering its effects as normal.

When an ally you can see within 30 feet of you casts a spell that affects one or more creatures other than itself, you can take a Reaction to cause that ally to gain the effects of the spell as well.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

If you try to use this feature against an effect caused by a **level 6 or higher spell**, the Reaction fails, but the use of this feature isn't expended.

## Sight Beyond Sight

_Level 17 Judgement Domain Feature_

Your eyes open to the ultimate truths of not only the living soul, but reality itself.

When you use your **Eyes of Truth** feature:

- The distance at which you perceive increases to **120 feet**.
    
- You gain **Truesight** within that area.
    
- The effect lasts for **10 minutes**.
    

In addition, the ranges of your **Pass Judgement** and **Wages of Sin** features increase to **120 feet**.
